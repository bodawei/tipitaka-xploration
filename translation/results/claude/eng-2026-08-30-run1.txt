Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 44-63

[44] "Should any bhikkhu, having undertaken the bhikkhus' training and way of life, and not having renounced the training nor declared his weakness, engage in sexual intercourse, even with a female animal, he is one who is defeated; he is not in communion."

[45] "Should any": whoever, of whatever sort, of whatever condition, of whatever birth, of whatever name, of whatever clan, of whatever conduct, of whatever manner of dwelling, of whatever resort — whether an elder, a newcomer, or of middle standing: this is what is called "should any." "Bhikkhu": he is a bhikkhu because he is a beggar; he is a bhikkhu because he has taken up the practice of going for alms; he is a bhikkhu because he wears cloth that has been cut up; he is a bhikkhu by common designation; he is a bhikkhu by his own acknowledgment; he is a bhikkhu by "Come, bhikkhu"; he is a bhikkhu by having been given the higher ordination through the three goings for refuge; he is a good bhikkhu, a bhikkhu of substance, a bhikkhu in training, a bhikkhu beyond training; he is a bhikkhu by having been given the higher ordination by a united Saṅgha through a formal act with a motion as fourth, an act that is unshakable and fit to stand. Herein, the bhikkhu who has been given the higher ordination by a united Saṅgha through a formal act with a motion as fourth, unshakable and fit to stand — this is the bhikkhu intended in this connection. "Training": there are three trainings — the training in higher virtue, the training in higher mind, the training in higher wisdom. Herein, the training in higher virtue is the training intended in this connection. "Way of life" means: whatever training rule has been laid down by the Blessed One, that is called the way of life. In that he trains; therefore it is said "having undertaken the way of life." "Not having renounced the training nor declared his weakness": there is, bhikkhus, a declaring of weakness where the training has not been renounced; there is, bhikkhus, a declaring of weakness where the training has been renounced. "And how, bhikkhus, is there a declaring of weakness where the training has not been renounced? Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu who is dissatisfied, discontented, wishing to fall away from the recluse's life, troubled by the state of being a bhikkhu, ashamed of it, disgusted with it, longing for the state of a householder, longing for the state of a lay follower, longing for the state of a monastery attendant, longing for the state of a novice, longing for the state of a sectarian, longing for the state of a sectarian's disciple, longing for the state of a non-recluse, longing for the state of one who is not a son of the Sakyan, says and makes known: 'What if I were to renounce the Buddha?' In this way too, bhikkhus, there is a declaring of weakness where the training has not been renounced. "Or else, being dissatisfied, discontented, wishing to fall away from the recluse's life, troubled by the state of being a bhikkhu, ashamed of it, disgusted with it, longing for the state of a householder … etc. … longing for the state of one who is not a son of the Sakyan, he says and makes known: 'What if I were to renounce the Dhamma?' … etc. … 'What if I were to renounce the Saṅgha?' … 'the training' … 'the Vinaya' … 'the Pātimokkha' … 'the recitation' … 'my preceptor' … 'my teacher' … 'my co-resident pupil' … 'my apprentice' … 'one who shares my preceptor' … 'one who shares my teacher' … 'What if I were to renounce my fellows in the holy life?' — so he says and makes known. He says and makes known: 'What if I were to be a householder?' 'What if I were to be a lay follower?' … 'What if I were to be a monastery attendant?' … 'What if I were to be a novice?' … 'What if I were to be a sectarian?' … 'What if I were to be a sectarian's disciple?' … 'What if I were to be a non-recluse?' … 'What if I were to be one who is not a son of the Sakyan?' — so he says and makes known. In this way too, bhikkhus, there is a declaring of weakness where the training has not been renounced.
