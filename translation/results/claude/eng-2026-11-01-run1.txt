[180] At that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. Out of compassion the bhikkhus praised the beauty of death to him. That bhikkhu died. "You, bhikkhus, have committed an offence entailing defeat." At that time a certain almsgoing bhikkhu, sitting down on a stool on which a child was lying covered by a cloth, crushed it and killed it.
