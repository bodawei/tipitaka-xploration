[215] By three modes: wishing to say "I attained the first jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the second jhāna" — if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three modes: wishing to say "I attained the first jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the third jhāna" — if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three modes: wishing to say "I attained the first jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the fourth jhāna" — if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three modes … and so on … by seven modes: wishing to say "I attained the first jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the empty liberation" … "the signless liberation" … "the undirected liberation" … "the empty concentration" … "the signless concentration" … "the undirected concentration" … "the empty attainment" … "the signless attainment" … "the undirected attainment" … "the three true knowledges" … "the four foundations of mindfulness" … "the four right strivings" … "the four bases of psychic power" … "the five faculties" … "the five powers" … "the seven factors of enlightenment" … "the noble eightfold path" … "the fruit of stream-entry" … "the fruit of once-returning" … "the fruit of non-returning" … "arahantship" … and so on … "Lust has been relinquished by me" … "Hatred has been relinquished by me" … "Delusion has been relinquished by me, vomited out, released, abandoned, given up, cast off, utterly cast off." "My mind is unobstructed by lust" … "My mind is unobstructed by hatred" … "My mind is unobstructed by delusion" — if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. Beforehand he thinks, "I shall speak falsely"; while speaking he thinks, "I am speaking falsely"; after speaking he thinks, "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.
