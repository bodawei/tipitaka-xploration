[219] By three factors, wishing to say, "I attained the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna and the empty liberation and the signless liberation and the undirected liberation and the empty concentration and the signless concentration and the undirected concentration and the empty attainment and the signless attainment and the undirected attainment and the three true knowledges and the four foundations of mindfulness and the four right strivings and the four bases of psychic power and the five faculties and the five powers and the seven factors of enlightenment and the noble eightfold path and the fruit of stream-entry and the fruit of once-returning and the fruit of non-returning and arahantship" … "and lust has been renounced by me … and hatred has been renounced by me … and delusion has been renounced by me, vomited up, let go, abandoned, relinquished, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust … and my mind is unobstructed by hatred … and my mind is unobstructed by delusion" — if he says, deliberately lying, "I attained the first jhāna," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three factors, wishing to say, "the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna … and my mind is unobstructed by delusion and I attained the first jhāna" — if he says, deliberately lying, "I attained the second jhāna," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three factors … by seven factors, wishing to say, "and my mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the first jhāna and the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna … and my mind is unobstructed by lust" — if he says, deliberately lying, "My mind is unobstructed by hatred," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence.
