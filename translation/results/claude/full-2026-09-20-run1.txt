Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 97-106

[97] "Suspended aloft" means goods that are up in the air. They may be hung on a bed, or a chair, or a robe-pole, or a robe-line, or a wall-peg, or an elephant-tusk peg, or a tree, or even on a bowl-stand. Thinking, "I will steal goods suspended aloft," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat.

[98] "Standing in water" means goods placed in water. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in water," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He dives in or surfaces: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. If, with thievish mind, he touches a blue lotus, or a red lotus, or a white lotus, or a lotus root, or a fish, or a turtle growing there, worth five māsakas or more than five māsakas: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat.

[99] "Boat" means that by which one crosses over. "Standing in a boat" means goods placed in a boat. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a boat," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. Thinking, "I will steal the boat," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He unties the mooring: an offence of wrong-doing. Having untied the mooring, he touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He moves it upstream, or downstream, or across, even by so much as a hair's breadth: an offence entailing defeat.

[100] "Vehicle" means a palanquin, a chariot, a cart, a carriage. "Standing in a vehicle" means goods placed in a vehicle. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a vehicle," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. Thinking, "I will steal the vehicle," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat.

[101] "Load" means a load on the head, a load on the shoulder, a load on the hip, a load carried hanging in the hand. If with thievish mind he touches a load on the head: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He lowers it to the shoulder: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he touches a load on the shoulder: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He lowers it to the hip: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he touches a load on the hip: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He takes it with the hand: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he puts down on the ground a load that is in the hand: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he takes it up from the ground: an offence entailing defeat.

[102] "Park" means a flower park, a fruit park. "Standing in a park" means goods placed in a park in any of four positions: resting on the ground, resting on a surface, standing in the open air, suspended aloft. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a park," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he touches a root, or bark, or a leaf, or a flower, or a fruit growing there, worth five māsakas or more than five māsakas: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. He lays claim to the park: an offence of wrong-doing. He arouses doubt in the owner: a grave offence. When the owner, thinking, "It will not be mine," gives up his claim: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he defeats the owner: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he is himself defeated: a grave offence.

[103] "Standing in a dwelling" means goods placed in a dwelling in any of four positions: resting on the ground, resting on a surface, standing in the open air, suspended aloft. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a dwelling," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. He lays claim to the dwelling: an offence of wrong-doing. He arouses doubt in the owner: a grave offence. When the owner, thinking, "It will not be mine," gives up his claim: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he defeats the owner: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he is himself defeated: a grave offence.

[104] "Field" means where grain crops or vegetable crops grow. "Standing in a field" means goods placed in a field in any of four positions: resting on the ground, resting on a surface, standing in the open air, suspended aloft. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a field," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. If with thievish mind he touches grain crops or vegetable crops growing there, worth five māsakas or more than five māsakas: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. He lays claim to the field: an offence of wrong-doing. He arouses doubt in the owner: a grave offence. When the owner, thinking, "It will not be mine," gives up his claim: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he defeats the owner: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he is himself defeated: a grave offence. If he shifts a stake, or a cord, or a fence, or a boundary marker: an offence of wrong-doing. While one further act remains to be done: a grave offence. When that act has been done: an offence entailing defeat.

[105] "Site" means a park site, a dwelling site. "Standing on a site" means goods placed on a site in any of four positions: resting on the ground, resting on a surface, standing in the open air, suspended aloft. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing on a site," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat. He lays claim to the site: an offence of wrong-doing. He arouses doubt in the owner: a grave offence. When the owner, thinking, "It will not be mine," gives up his claim: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he defeats the owner: an offence entailing defeat. If, going to law, he is himself defeated: a grave offence. If he shifts a stake, or a cord, or a fence, or a wall: an offence of wrong-doing. While one further act remains to be done: a grave offence. When that act has been done: an offence entailing defeat.

[106] "Standing in a village" means goods placed in a village in any of four positions: resting on the ground, resting on a surface, standing in the open air, suspended aloft. Thinking, "I will steal goods standing in a village," with thievish mind he looks for an accomplice or goes: an offence of wrong-doing. He touches it: an offence of wrong-doing. He makes it shake: a grave offence. He shifts it from its place: an offence entailing defeat.

Traditional Chinese:

[97] 「空中懸置物」者，謂物品置於空中：掛於床、椅、衣桁、衣繩、壁釘、象牙釘、樹上者，乃至掛於鉢架上者。以盜心念「我當取此空中懸置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。

[98] 「水中置物」者，謂物品置於水中。以盜心念「我當取此水中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。潛入水中或浮出水面者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。若於彼水中所生之青蓮、紅蓮、白蓮、藕根、魚、龜，值五摩沙迦或過五摩沙迦者，以盜心觸摸，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。

[99] 「船」者，謂依之渡水者。「船中置物」者，謂物品置於船中。以盜心念「我當取此船中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。以盜心念「我當取此船」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。解其繫縛者，突吉羅。解縛已而觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。若向上、向下、或橫向移動，乃至如一髮端之量，波羅夷。

[100] 「車乘」者，謂轎輦、車、貨車、輦輿。「車乘中置物」者，謂物品置於車乘中。以盜心念「我當取此車乘中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。以盜心念「我當取此車乘」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。

[101] 「擔」者，謂頭上擔、肩上擔、腰上擔、手中懸提之擔。以盜心觸摸頭上之擔者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移置於肩者，波羅夷。以盜心觸摸肩上之擔者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移置於腰者，波羅夷。以盜心觸摸腰上之擔者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。以手取之者，波羅夷。手中之擔，以盜心置於地上者，波羅夷。以盜心從地上取之者，波羅夷。

[102] 「園」者，謂花園、果園。「園中置物」者，謂物品以四種處所置於園中——置於地上、置於陸地、置於空中、懸置空中。以盜心念「我當取此園中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。若於彼園中所生之根、皮、葉、花、果，值五摩沙迦或過五摩沙迦者，以盜心觸摸，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。訴求爭奪其園者，突吉羅。令園主生疑惑者，偷蘭遮。園主念「此園將不屬於我」而捨棄其權者，波羅夷。興訟而令園主敗訴者，波羅夷。興訟而自己敗訴者，偷蘭遮。

[103] 「精舍中置物」者，謂物品以四種處所置於精舍中——置於地上、置於陸地、置於空中、懸置空中。以盜心念「我當取此精舍中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。訴求爭奪其精舍者，突吉羅。令屋主生疑惑者，偷蘭遮。屋主念「此將不屬於我」而捨棄其權者，波羅夷。興訟而令屋主敗訴者，波羅夷。興訟而自己敗訴者，偷蘭遮。

[104] 「田」者，謂生長穀類或豆類等作物之處。「田中置物」者，謂物品以四種處所置於田中——置於地上、置於陸地、置於空中、懸置空中。以盜心念「我當取此田中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。若於彼田中所生之穀類或豆類等，值五摩沙迦或過五摩沙迦者，以盜心觸摸，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。訴求爭奪其田者，突吉羅。令田主生疑惑者，偷蘭遮。田主念「此田將不屬於我」而捨棄其權者，波羅夷。興訟而令田主敗訴者，波羅夷。興訟而自己敗訴者，偷蘭遮。若移動界樁、界繩、籬笆或界限者，突吉羅。尚餘一加行未至時，偷蘭遮。至彼加行時，波羅夷。

[105] 「地」者，謂園地、精舍地。「地上置物」者，謂物品以四種處所置於其地——置於地上、置於陸地、置於空中、懸置空中。以盜心念「我當取此地上所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。訴求爭奪其地者，突吉羅。令地主生疑惑者，偷蘭遮。地主念「此地將不屬於我」而捨棄其權者，波羅夷。興訟而令地主敗訴者，波羅夷。興訟而自己敗訴者，偷蘭遮。若移動界樁、界繩、籬笆或牆垣者，突吉羅。尚餘一加行未至時，偷蘭遮。至彼加行時，波羅夷。

[106] 「村中置物」者，謂物品以四種處所置於村中——置於地上、置於陸地、置於空中、懸置空中。以盜心念「我當取此村中所置之物」而尋覓同伴或前往者，突吉羅。觸摸者，突吉羅。令動搖者，偷蘭遮。移離本處者，波羅夷。

[105] Vatthu nāma ārāmavatthu vihāravatthu. Vatthuṭṭhaṃ nāma bhaṇḍaṃ vatthusmiṃ catūhi ṭhānehi nikkhittaṃ hoti – bhūmaṭṭhaṃ, thalaṭṭhaṃ, ākāsaṭṭhaṃ, vehāsaṭṭhaṃ. Vatthuṭṭhaṃ bhaṇḍaṃ avaharissāmīti theyyacitto dutiyaṃ vā pariyesati gacchati vā, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Āmasati, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Phandāpeti, āpatti thullaccayassa. Ṭhānā cāveti, āpatti pārājikassa. Vatthuṃ abhiyuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Sāmikassa vimatiṃ uppādeti, āpatti thullaccayassa. Sāmiko na mayhaṃ bhavissatīti dhuraṃ nikkhipati, āpatti pārājikassa. Dhammaṃ caranto sāmikaṃ parājeti, āpatti pārājikassa. Dhammaṃ caranto parajjati, āpatti thullaccayassa. Khīlaṃ vā rajjuṃ vā vatiṃ vā pākāraṃ vā saṅkāmeti, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Ekaṃ payogaṃ anāgate āpatti thullaccayassa. Tasmiṃ payoge āgate āpatti pārājikassa.

Gāmaṭṭhaṃ bhaṇḍaṃ avaharissāmīti theyyacitto dutiyaṃ vā pariyesati gacchati vā, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Āmasati, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Phandāpeti, āpatti thullaccayassa. Ṭhānā cāveti, āpatti pārājikassa.
