Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 152-161

[152] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was travelling along the highroad together with a caravan. A certain man, having won that bhikkhu over with a gift of goods, when he saw the toll-station gave him a costly jewel, saying: "Venerable sir, carry this jewel past the toll-station." Then that bhikkhu carried the jewel past the toll-station. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat."

[153] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, out of compassion, released a pig that was caught in a snare. He became remorseful … "With what intention did you act, bhikkhu?" "I acted out of compassion, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who acts out of compassion." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu released a pig caught in a snare with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see it." He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, out of compassion, released a deer caught in a snare … released a deer caught in a snare with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see it" … out of compassion released fish caught in a fish-trap … released fish caught in a fish-trap with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see it." He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, seeing goods in a cart, thought, "If I take from here I shall be one defeated," and, going on ahead, he made the goods roll off and took them. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu took a piece of meat that had been carried up by a hawk, thinking, "I shall give it to the owners." The owners reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who has no thievish mind." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu took a piece of meat that had been carried up by a hawk with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see it." The owners reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat."

[154] Now at that time people, having bound together a raft, were floating it down the river Aciravatī. The binding broke and the logs went drifting apart. Bhikkhus, perceiving them as rags from a dust-heap, pulled them out. The owners reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one who perceives it as a rag from a dust-heap." Now at that time people, having bound together a raft, were floating it down the river Aciravatī. The binding broke and the logs went drifting apart. Bhikkhus with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see them," pulled them out. The owners reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … "Bhikkhus, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain cowherd hung his cloth on a tree and went to relieve himself. A certain bhikkhu, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, took it. Then that cowherd reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who perceives it as a rag from a dust-heap." Now at that time, as a certain bhikkhu was crossing a river, a cloth that had slipped from the hands of washermen caught on his foot. That bhikkhu took it, thinking, "I shall give it to the owners." The owners reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who has no thievish mind." Now at that time, as a certain bhikkhu was crossing a river, a cloth that had slipped from the hands of washermen caught on his foot. That bhikkhu took it with a thievish mind, thinking, "Before the owners see it." The owners reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat."

[155] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, finding a jar of ghee, consumed it little by little. He became remorseful … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time several bhikkhus went off by arrangement, thinking, "We shall steal goods." One of them stole the goods. They said: "We are not defeated; he who stole them is defeated." They reported the matter to the Blessed One … "Bhikkhus, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time several bhikkhus by arrangement stole goods and divided them up. When they had divided them, each one's portion did not amount to five māsakas. They said: "We are not defeated." They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "Bhikkhus, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time, during a famine at Sāvatthī, a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind stole a handful of rice from a shopkeeper. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time, during a famine at Sāvatthī, a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind stole from a shopkeeper a handful of mung beans … a handful of beans … a handful of sesame. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time at Sāvatthī, in the Dark Wood, some thieves killed a cow, ate the flesh, put aside the remainder, and went off. Bhikkhus, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, had it formally offered to them and ate it. The thieves reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one who perceives it as a rag from a dust-heap." Now at that time at Sāvatthī, in the Dark Wood, some thieves killed a pig, ate the flesh, put aside the remainder, and went off. Bhikkhus, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, had it formally offered to them and ate it. The thieves reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one who perceives it as a rag from a dust-heap." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu went to a grass field and with a thievish mind stole cut grass worth five māsakas. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu went to a grass field, cut grass, and with a thievish mind stole it, worth five māsakas. He became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat."

[156] Now at that time incoming bhikkhus had the Saṅgha's mangoes distributed and ate them. The resident bhikkhus reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … and reported the matter to the Blessed One. "With what intention did you act, bhikkhus?" "For the sake of consuming them, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhus, when it is for the sake of consuming them." Now at that time incoming bhikkhus had the Saṅgha's rose-apples … the Saṅgha's breadfruit … the Saṅgha's jackfruit … the Saṅgha's palm-fruit … the Saṅgha's sugarcane … the Saṅgha's timbaru fruit distributed and ate them. The resident bhikkhus reproached those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They became remorseful … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, when it is for the sake of consuming them." Now at that time the keepers of the mango orchard gave mango fruit to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus, being scrupulous, did not accept it, thinking, "These men have authority to guard, not to give." They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, in a gift made by a keeper." Now at that time the keepers of rose-apples … the keepers of breadfruit … the keepers of jackfruit … the keepers of palm-fruit … the keepers of sugarcane … the keepers of timbaru fruit gave timbaru fruit to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus, being scrupulous, did not accept it, thinking, "These men have authority to guard, not to give." They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, in a gift made by a keeper." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu took the Saṅgha's timber as a temporary borrowing and propped up the wall of his own dwelling with it. Bhikkhus reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He became remorseful. He reported the matter to the Blessed One. "With what intention did you act, bhikkhu?" "It was a temporary borrowing, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, in a temporary borrowing." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind stole the Saṅgha's water … with a thievish mind stole the Saṅgha's clay … with a thievish mind stole the Saṅgha's heaped-up grass … he became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind burned the Saṅgha's heaped-up grass. He became remorseful … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind stole the Saṅgha's bed … he became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu with a thievish mind stole the Saṅgha's chair … the Saṅgha's mattress … the Saṅgha's pillow … the Saṅgha's door … the Saṅgha's window … the Saṅgha's rafter … he became remorseful … "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat."

[157] Now at that time bhikkhus used elsewhere furniture belonging to a certain lay follower which was meant for use in one dwelling. Then that lay follower looked down upon it, criticised it, and complained: "How can the venerable ones use elsewhere what is meant for use in one place!" They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "Bhikkhus, what is meant for use in one place should not be used elsewhere. Whoever should so use it, there is an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time bhikkhus, being scrupulous about carrying it even to the uposatha hall or to the assembly place, sat on the bare ground, and their limbs and their robes became soiled with dust. They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "I allow you, bhikkhus, to take it as a temporary borrowing." Now at that time at Campā a bhikkhunī who was a pupil of the bhikkhunī Thullanandā went to a family that supported the bhikkhunī Thullanandā and said, "The noble lady wishes to drink gruel made with the three pungents," and having had it cooked and carried it off, she consumed it herself. When Thullanandā found out, she reproached her: "You are no recluse-woman." She became remorseful. Then that bhikkhunī reported the matter to the bhikkhunīs. The bhikkhunīs reported the matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is an offence of expiation for deliberate lying." Now at that time at Rājagaha a bhikkhunī who was a pupil of the bhikkhunī Thullanandā went to a family that supported the bhikkhunī Thullanandā and said, "The noble lady wishes to eat a honey-ball," and having had it made and carried it off, she consumed it herself. When Thullanandā found out, she reproached her: "You are no recluse-woman." She became remorseful … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is an offence of expiation for deliberate lying."

[158] Now at that time at Vesālī the householder who supported the venerable Ajjuka had two boys, a son and a nephew. Then that householder said this to the venerable Ajjuka: "Venerable sir, point out this place to whichever of these two boys is faithful and devout." Now at that time that householder's nephew was faithful and devout. Then the venerable Ajjuka pointed out that place to that boy. With that wealth he established a household and instituted a charity. Then that householder's son said this to the venerable Ānanda: "Venerable Ānanda, who is the heir of a father — the son or the nephew?" "The son, friend, is the father's heir." "Venerable sir, this master Ajjuka pointed out our wealth to our kinsman." "The venerable Ajjuka is no recluse, friend." Then the venerable Ajjuka said this to the venerable Ānanda: "Friend Ānanda, give me a judgment." Now at that time the venerable Upāli took the side of the venerable Ajjuka. Then the venerable Upāli said this to the venerable Ānanda: "Friend Ānanda, if someone is told by an owner, 'Point out this place to so-and-so,' and he points it out to him, what offence does he incur?" "He incurs nothing at all, venerable sir, not even so much as an offence of wrong-doing." "This venerable Ajjuka, friend, was told by the owner, 'Point out this place to so-and-so,' and he pointed it out to him; there is no offence, friend, for the venerable Ajjuka."

[159] Now at that time at Bārāṇasī a family that supported the venerable Pilindavaccha was harassed by thieves, and two children were carried off. Then the venerable Pilindavaccha brought those children back by psychic power and set them in the mansion. When people saw those children they said, "This is the might of master Pilindavaccha's psychic power," and they became deeply devoted to the venerable Pilindavaccha. Bhikkhus looked down upon it, criticised it, and complained: "How can the venerable Pilindavaccha bring back children who had been carried off by thieves!" They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one of psychic power in the range of psychic power."

[160] Now at that time two bhikkhus were friends, Paṇḍuka and Kapila. One was living in a village, the other at Kosambī. Then, as that bhikkhu was going from the village to Kosambī and was crossing a river on the way, a roll of fat that had slipped from the hands of pork-butchers caught on his foot. That bhikkhu took it, thinking, "I shall give it to the owners." The owners reproached that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." When he had come out of the water, a cowherd woman saw him and said this: "Come, venerable sir, engage in sexual intercourse." Thinking, "I am no recluse anyway," he engaged in sexual intercourse with her, went to Kosambī, and reported the matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat for taking what is not given, bhikkhus; there is an offence entailing defeat for engaging in sexual intercourse."

[161] Now at that time at Sāgalā a bhikkhu who shared the venerable Daḷhika's quarters, afflicted by discontent, stole a shopkeeper's turban and said this to the venerable Daḷhika: "Venerable sir, I am no recluse; I shall leave the Order." "What have you done, friend?" He reported the matter. Daḷhika had the turban brought and had it valued. On being valued it was not worth five māsakas. "There is no offence entailing defeat, friend." He gave him a talk on the Dhamma, and that bhikkhu found delight in the life again.

[152] 爾時，有一比丘與商隊同行於道路。有一男子以財物籠絡該比丘，見有稅關處，便以一貴重寶珠交與該比丘，言：「大德，請將此寶珠帶過稅關。」於是該比丘便將此寶珠帶過稅關處。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」

[153] 爾時，有一比丘以憐愍心，釋放陷於羅網之豬。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝是何心？」「世尊，我是出於憐愍之意。」「比丘，出於憐愍之意者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘見陷於羅網之豬，心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心而放之。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘以憐愍心釋放陷於羅網之鹿……見陷於羅網之鹿，心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心而放之……以憐愍心釋放落入魚笱之魚……見落入魚笱之魚，心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心而放之。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘見車上之貨物，心想「我若自此取之，則成波羅夷」，乃先行越過，使貨物滾落而後取之。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘見鷹所攫去之肉片，心想「我當還與其主」而取之。物主責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，無盜心者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘見鷹所攫去之肉片，心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心而取之。物主責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」

[154] 爾時，諸人縛結木筏，放流於阿夷羅跋提河中。縛結斷裂，木材散落漂流而去。諸比丘作糞掃衣想而撈取上岸。物主責諸比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，作糞掃物想者無罪。」爾時，諸人縛結木筏，放流於阿夷羅跋提河中。縛結斷裂，木材散落漂流而去。諸比丘心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心撈取上岸。物主責諸比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，汝等已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一牧牛者將衣掛於樹上而去大小便。有一比丘作糞掃衣想而取之。時彼牧牛者責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，作糞掃衣想者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘渡河時，自染衣者手中漂失之衣掛於其足。該比丘心想「我當還與其主」而取之。物主責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，無盜心者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘渡河時，自染衣者手中漂失之衣掛於其足。該比丘心想「趁主人未見之前」，以盜心而取之。物主責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」

[155] 爾時，有一比丘見一甕酥，少少取食之。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，不犯波羅夷，犯突吉羅。」爾時，眾多比丘共謀而往，言：「我等當竊取財物。」其中一人竊取財物。彼等如是言：「我等非波羅夷，唯行竊者乃波羅夷。」諸比丘以此事白世尊……「諸比丘，汝等已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，眾多比丘共謀竊取財物而後分配。分配之時，各人所得不滿五摩沙迦。彼等如是言：「我等非波羅夷。」以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，汝等已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，舍衛城饑饉之時，有一比丘以盜心竊取店主之一把米。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，舍衛城饑饉之時，有一比丘以盜心竊取店主之一把綠豆……一把豆……一把胡麻。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，舍衛城安陀林中，諸賊殺牛食肉，將餘肉藏置而去。諸比丘作糞掃物想，令人授與而食之。諸賊責諸比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，作糞掃物想者無罪。」爾時，舍衛城安陀林中，諸賊殺豬食肉，將餘肉藏置而去。諸比丘作糞掃物想，令人授與而食之。諸賊責諸比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，作糞掃物想者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘往草田，以盜心竊取已刈之草值五摩沙迦者。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘往草田，自刈其草，以盜心竊取值五摩沙迦者。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」

[156] 爾時，客比丘將僧伽之芒果令人分配而食之。舊住比丘責彼等比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，汝等是何心？」「世尊，我等為受用之故。」「諸比丘，為受用之故者無罪。」爾時，客比丘將僧伽之閻浮果……僧伽之婆那娑果……僧伽之波那娑果……僧伽之多羅果……僧伽之甘蔗……僧伽之帝目樓沙果，令人分配而食之。舊住比丘責彼等比丘言：「汝等非沙門！」彼等心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，為受用之故者無罪。」爾時，守芒果園者以芒果施與諸比丘。諸比丘心想：「此等人唯有看守之權，無施與之權」，懷追悔而不受。以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，受守園人之施與者無罪。」爾時，守閻浮樹者……守婆那娑樹者……守波那娑樹者……守多羅樹者……守甘蔗田者……守帝目樓沙樹者，以帝目樓沙果施與諸比丘。諸比丘心想：「此等人唯有看守之權，無施與之權」，懷追悔而不受。以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，受守園人之施與者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘暫時取用僧伽之木材，持去支撐自己住處之牆壁。諸比丘責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼心生追悔，以此事白世尊。「比丘，汝是何心？」「世尊，我是暫時取用。」「比丘，暫時取用者無罪。」爾時，有一比丘以盜心竊取僧伽之水……以盜心竊取僧伽之泥土……以盜心竊取僧伽所積聚之草……彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘以盜心焚燒僧伽所積聚之草。彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，不犯波羅夷，犯突吉羅。」爾時，有一比丘以盜心竊取僧伽之床……彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」爾時，有一比丘以盜心竊取僧伽之椅……僧伽之褥……僧伽之枕……僧伽之門扉……僧伽之窗……僧伽之椽木……彼心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，汝已犯波羅夷罪。」

[157] 爾時，諸比丘將某優婆塞施與某一住處受用之臥坐具，移往他處受用。時彼優婆塞譏嫌、非難、責備曰：「諸大德何以將供於此處受用者，移於他處受用耶！」以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，不應將供於此處受用者移於他處受用。若受用者，犯突吉羅。」爾時，諸比丘往布薩堂及集會處時，懷追悔不敢攜帶臥坐具，乃坐於地上，身體與衣皆沾塵土。以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，我聽許暫時取用攜往。」爾時，於瞻波城，偷蘭難陀比丘尼之弟子比丘尼，往偷蘭難陀比丘尼之護持家，言：「聖尼欲飲三味粥」，令彼煮之，取回而自食之。偷蘭難陀知已，責彼言：「汝非沙門尼！」彼心生追悔。時該比丘尼以此事告諸比丘尼，諸比丘尼以此事告諸比丘，諸比丘以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，不犯波羅夷，犯故妄語波逸提。」爾時，於王舍城，偷蘭難陀比丘尼之弟子比丘尼，往偷蘭難陀比丘尼之護持家，言：「聖尼欲食蜜丸」，令彼作之，取回而自食之。偷蘭難陀知已，責彼言：「汝非沙門尼！」彼心生追悔……乃至……「諸比丘，不犯波羅夷，犯故妄語波逸提。」

[158] 爾時，於毘舍離，具壽阿豎迦之護持者居士有二童子——一為其子，一為其外甥。時彼居士告具壽阿豎迦曰：「大德，此二童子之中，若有信心、淨信者，請將此藏處告知於彼。」爾時，彼居士之外甥有信心、有淨信。於是具壽阿豎迦將彼藏處告知該童子。彼以其財物建立家業，並行布施。時彼居士之子問具壽阿難曰：「大德阿難，父之遺產應屬何人——子耶？外甥耶？」「賢友，子乃父之繼承人。」「大德，此阿豎迦尊者竟將我等之財物告知我等之姻親！」「賢友，具壽阿豎迦非沙門也。」時具壽阿豎迦告具壽阿難曰：「賢友阿難，請為我作判決。」爾時，具壽優波離為具壽阿豎迦之同伴。時具壽優波離告具壽阿難曰：「賢友阿難，若物主告人言『汝當將此藏處告知某甲』，而彼即告知其人，則彼犯何罪？」「大德，不犯任何罪，乃至突吉羅亦無。」「賢友，此具壽阿豎迦正是受物主之囑『當將此藏處告知某甲』而告知其人，故賢友，具壽阿豎迦無罪。」

[159] 爾時，於波羅奈，具壽畢陵伽婆蹉之護持家為賊所劫，二童子被擄去。時具壽畢陵伽婆蹉以神通將彼二童子攜回，置於樓閣之上。諸人見此二童子，言：「此乃畢陵伽婆蹉尊者之神通威力！」於具壽畢陵伽婆蹉深生淨信。諸比丘譏嫌、非難、責備曰：「具壽畢陵伽婆蹉何以將賊所擄去之童子攜回耶！」以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，有神通者於神通境界中無罪。」

[160] 爾時，有二比丘互為友伴——般荼迦與迦毘羅。一人住於村落，一人住於憍賞彌。時住村落之比丘往憍賞彌，中途渡河，自屠豬者手中漂失之脂肪塊掛於其足。該比丘心想「我當還與其主」而取之。物主責該比丘言：「汝非沙門！」彼上岸時，一牧牛女見之，告彼曰：「大德，來與我行淫法。」彼心想「我本已非沙門」，遂與彼女行淫法，至憍賞彌後以此事告諸比丘。諸比丘以此事白世尊。「諸比丘，於不與取不犯波羅夷；於交會行淫法犯波羅夷。」

[161] 爾時，於舍伽羅，具壽達利迦之共住弟子比丘為不樂所逼，竊取店主之頭巾，而告具壽達利迦曰：「大德，我非沙門矣，我將還俗。」「賢友，汝作何事？」彼即以其事相告。達利迦令人取來估價，估之不值五摩沙迦。「賢友，不犯波羅夷。」乃為彼說法。彼比丘遂生歡喜樂住。

Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu saṅghassa mañcaṃ theyyacitto avahari… tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu saṅghassa pīṭhaṃ… saṅghassa bhisiṃ… saṅghassa bibbohanaṃ … saṅghassa kavāṭaṃ… saṅghassa ālokasandhiṃ… saṅghassa gopānasiṃ theyyacitto avahari… tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti.

[157] Tena kho pana samayena bhikkhū aññatarassa upāsakassa vihāraparibhogaṃ senāsanaṃ aññatra paribhuñjanti. Atha kho so upāsako ujjhāyati khiyyati vipāceti – ‘‘kathañhi nāma bhadantā aññatra paribhogaṃ aññatra paribhuñjissantī’’ti! Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Na, bhikkhave, aññatra paribhogo aññatra paribhuñjitabbo. Yo paribhuñjeyya, āpatti dukkaṭassā’’ti. Tena kho pana samayena bhikkhū uposathaggampi sannisajjampi harituṃ kukkuccāyantā chamāyaṃ nisīdanti. Gattānipi cīvarānipi paṃsukitāni honti. Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Anujānāmi, bhikkhave, tāvakālikaṃ haritu’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena campāyaṃ thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā antevāsinī bhikkhunī thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā upaṭṭhākakulaṃ gantvā – ‘‘ayyā icchati tekaṭulayāguṃ pātu’’nti, pacāpetvā haritvā attanā paribhuñji. Sā jānitvā taṃ codesi – ‘‘assamaṇīsi tva’’nti. Tassā kukkuccaṃ ahosi. Atha kho sā bhikkhunī bhikkhunīnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesi. Bhikkhuniyo bhikkhūnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti sampajānamusāvāde pācittiyassā’’ti. Tena kho pana samayena rājagahe thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā antevāsinī bhikkhunī thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā upaṭṭhākakulaṃ gantvā – ‘‘ayyā icchati madhugoḷakaṃ khāditu’’nti, pacāpetvā haritvā attanā paribhuñji. Sā jānitvā taṃ codesi – ‘‘assamaṇīsi tva’’nti. Tassā kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti sampajānamusāvāde pācittiyassā’’ti.

[158] Tena kho pana samayena vesāliyaṃ āyasmato ajjukassa upaṭṭhākassa gahapatino dve dārakā honti – putto ca bhāgineyyo ca. Atha kho so gahapati āyasmantaṃ ajjukaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘imaṃ, bhante, okāsaṃ yo imesaṃ dvinnaṃ dārakānaṃ saddho hoti pasanno tassa ācikkheyyāsī’’ti . Tena kho pana samayena tassa gahapatino bhāgineyyo saddho hoti pasanno. Atha kho āyasmā ajjuko taṃ okāsaṃ tassa dārakassa ācikkhi. So tena sāpateyyena kuṭumbañca saṇṭhapesi dānañca paṭṭhapesi. Atha kho tassa gahapatino putto āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘ko nu kho, bhante ānanda, pituno dāyajjo – putto vā bhāgineyyo vā’’ti? ‘‘Putto kho, āvuso, pituno dāyajjo’’ti. ‘‘Ayaṃ, bhante, ayyo ajjuko amhākaṃ sāpateyyaṃ amhākaṃ methunakassa ācikkhī’’ti. ‘‘Assamaṇo, āvuso, āyasmā ajjuko’’ti. Atha kho āyasmā ajjuko āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘dehi me, āvuso ānanda, vinicchaya’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā upāli āyasmato ajjukassa pakkho hoti. Atha kho āyasmā upāli āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘yo nu kho, āvuso ānanda, sāmikena ‘imaṃ okāsaṃ itthannāmassa ācikkheyyāsī’ti vutto tassa ācikkhati, kiṃ so āpajjatī’’ti? ‘‘Na, bhante, kiñci āpajjati, antamaso dukkaṭamattampī’’ti. ‘‘Ayaṃ, āvuso, āyasmā ajjuko sāmikena – ‘imaṃ okāsaṃ itthannāmassa ācikkhā’ti vutto tassa ācikkhati; anāpatti, āvuso, āyasmato ajjukassā’’ti.

[159] Tena kho pana samayena bārāṇasiyaṃ āyasmato pilindavacchassa upaṭṭhākakulaṃ corehi upaddutaṃ hoti. Dve ca dārakā nītā honti. Atha kho āyasmā pilindavaccho te dārake iddhiyā ānetvā pāsāde ṭhapesi. Manussā te dārake passitvā – ‘‘ayyassāyaṃ pilindavacchassa iddhānubhāvo’’ti, āyasmante pilindavacche abhippasīdiṃsu. Bhikkhū ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti – ‘‘kathañhi nāma āyasmā pilindavaccho corehi nīte dārake ānessatī’’ti! Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave , iddhimassa iddhivisaye’’ti.

[160] Tena kho pana samayena dve bhikkhū sahāyakā honti – paṇḍuko ca kapilo ca. Eko gāmake viharati, eko kosambiyaṃ. Atha kho tassa bhikkhuno gāmakā kosambiṃ gacchantassa antarāmagge nadiṃ tarantassa sūkarikānaṃ hatthato muttā medavaṭṭi pāde laggā hoti. So bhikkhu – ‘‘sāmikānaṃ dassāmī’’ti aggahesi. Sāmikā taṃ bhikkhuṃ codesuṃ – ‘‘assamaṇosi tva’’nti. Taṃ uttiṇṇaṃ gopālikā passitvā etadavoca – ‘‘ehi, bhante, methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevā’’ti. So – ‘‘pakatiyāpāhaṃ assamaṇo’’ti tassā methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevitvā kosambiṃ gantvā bhikkhūnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesi. Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ orocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave, adinnādāne pārājikassa; āpatti methunadhammasamāyoge pārājikassā’’ti.

[161] Tena kho pana samayena sāgalāyaṃ āyasmato daḷhikassa saddhivihāriko bhikkhu anabhiratiyā pīḷito āpaṇikassa veṭhanaṃ avaharitvā āyasmantaṃ daḷhikaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘assamaṇo ahaṃ, bhante, vibbhamissāmī’’ti. ‘‘Kiṃ tayā, āvuso, kata’’nti? So tamatthaṃ ārocesi. Āharāpetvā agghāpesi. Taṃ agghāpentaṃ na pañcamāsake agghati. ‘‘Anāpatti, āvuso, pārājikassā’’ti. Dhammakathaṃ akāsi. So bhikkhu abhiramatīti .
