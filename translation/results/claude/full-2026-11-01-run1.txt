[180] At that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. Out of compassion the bhikkhus praised the beauty of death to him. That bhikkhu died. They became remorseful: "A training rule has been laid down by the Blessed One — could it be that we have committed an offence entailing defeat?" They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "You, bhikkhus, have committed an offence entailing defeat." At that time a certain almsgoing bhikkhu, sitting down on a stool on which a child was lying covered by a cloth, crushed it and killed it. He became remorseful: "A training rule has been laid down by the Blessed One — could it be that I have committed an offence entailing defeat?" He reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu. But, bhikkhus, one should not sit down on a seat without first looking it over; whoever sits down thus commits an offence of wrong-doing." At that time a certain bhikkhu, while arranging a seat in the dining area inside a house, took hold of one of two pestles that were standing propped up. The second pestle fell down and struck the head of a certain child. The child died. He became remorseful… "With what mind did you act, bhikkhu?" "I did not act deliberately, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who acted unintentionally." At that time a certain bhikkhu, while arranging a seat in the dining area inside a house, stepped on a mortar-block and rolled it over. It crushed a certain child and killed it. He became remorseful… "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who acted unintentionally." At that time a father and a son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father, "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and taking him by the back he pushed him forward. He fell down and died. He became remorseful… "With what mind did you act, bhikkhu?" "I had no intention of causing death, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who had no intention of causing death." At that time a father and a son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father, "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death he took him by the back and pushed him forward. He fell down and died. He became remorseful… "You, bhikkhu, have committed an offence entailing defeat." At that time a father and a son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father, "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death he took him by the back and pushed him forward. He fell down but did not die. He became remorseful… "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is a grave offence."

[180] 那時，有一位比丘生病。諸比丘出於憐憫，向他讚歎死之美好。那位比丘便命終了。他們心生追悔：「世尊已制學處，我們是否犯了波羅夷罪？」他們將此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「諸比丘，你們所犯是波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位行乞食的比丘，要坐在一張椅子上，椅上有以布覆蓋的嬰兒，他壓在其上而致其死。他心生追悔：「世尊已制學處，我是否犯了波羅夷罪？」他將此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「比丘，不犯波羅夷。然而，諸比丘，不應未檢視而坐於座上；若坐者，犯突吉羅。」那時，有一位比丘在食堂內屋中鋪設座位，有兩根杵立著，他取其中一根杵。第二根杵倒落，砸在一個小孩頭上，那小孩便命終了。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你是何心？」「世尊，我是非故意的。」「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘在食堂內屋中鋪設座位，踏到臼具而使之滾動，壓在一個小孩身上而致其死。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，有父子二人同在比丘中出家。到了告知時間時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧伽正等著你呢。」便抓住他的背推了一把。他跌倒而命終。那人心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你是何心？」「世尊，我無致死之意圖。」「比丘，無致死之意圖者無罪。」那時，有父子二人同在比丘中出家。到了告知時間時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧伽正等著你呢。」懷著致死之意圖抓住他的背推了一把。他跌倒而命終。那人心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你所犯是波羅夷罪。」那時，有父子二人同在比丘中出家。到了告知時間時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧伽正等著你呢。」懷著致死之意圖抓住他的背推了一把。他雖跌倒卻未命終。那人心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，不犯波羅夷；犯偷蘭遮。」
