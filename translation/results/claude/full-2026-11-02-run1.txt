Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 190-192

[190] Now at that time the bhikkhus of the group of six set fire to a forest; people were burned and died. They felt remorse… "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one who had no intention of causing death." Now at that time the bhikkhus of the group of six, with the intention of causing death, set fire to a forest. People were burned and died… people were burned but did not die. They felt remorse… "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is a grave offence."

[191] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu went to the execution ground and said this to the executioner: "Friend, do not torment this man. Deprive him of life with a single blow." "Very well, venerable sir," he said, and deprived him of life with a single blow. He felt remorse… "You, bhikkhu, have committed an offence entailing defeat."

Now at that time a certain bhikkhu went to the execution ground and said this to the executioner: "Friend, do not torment this man. Deprive him of life with a single blow." Saying, "I will not do as you say," he deprived him of life. He felt remorse… "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is an offence of wrong-doing."

[192] Now at that time a certain man whose hands and feet had been cut off was in the house of his relatives, surrounded by his kinsfolk. A certain bhikkhu said this to those people: "Friends, do you wish for this man's death?" "Yes, venerable sir, we do wish it." "Then give him buttermilk to drink." They gave him buttermilk to drink. He died. The bhikkhu felt remorse… "You, bhikkhu, have committed an offence entailing defeat."

Now at that time a certain man whose hands and feet had been cut off was in a family house, surrounded by his kinsfolk. A certain bhikkhunī said this to those people: "Friends, do you wish for this man's death?" "Yes, noble lady, we do wish it." "Then give him salted sour gruel to drink." They gave him salted sour gruel to drink. He died. She felt remorse. Then that bhikkhunī reported this matter to the bhikkhunīs. The bhikkhunīs reported this matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported this matter to the Blessed One. "That bhikkhunī, bhikkhus, has committed an offence entailing defeat."

Traditional Chinese:

[190] 那時，六群比丘放火燒林；有人被燒死。他們心生追悔……乃至……「比丘們，無致死之意圖者，無罪。」那時，六群比丘以致死之意圖放火燒林。有人被燒而死……乃至……有人被燒而未死。他們心生追悔……乃至……「比丘們，不犯波羅夷；犯偷蘭遮。」

[191] 那時，有一比丘前往刑場，對劊子手這樣說：「賢友，別使此人受苦。一擊奪其命吧。」「好的，大德。」便一擊奪其命。那比丘心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一比丘前往刑場，對劊子手這樣說：「賢友，別使此人受苦。一擊奪其命吧。」那人說：「我不照你的話做。」便奪其命。那比丘心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，不犯波羅夷；犯突吉羅。」

[192] 那時，有一男子在親屬家中手足被截斷，為親屬們圍繞著。有一比丘對那些人這樣說：「賢友們，你們希望此人死嗎？」「是的，大德，我們希望。」「那麼就給他喝酪漿吧。」他們便給他喝酪漿。他就死了。那比丘心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一男子在俗家中手足被截斷，為親屬們圍繞著。有一比丘尼對那些人這樣說：「賢友們，你們希望此人死嗎？」「是的，聖尼，我們希望。」「那麼就給他喝鹽酸漿吧。」他們便給他喝鹽酸漿。他就死了。那比丘尼心生追悔。於是那比丘尼把此事告訴眾比丘尼。比丘尼們把此事告訴眾比丘。比丘們把此事稟告世尊。「比丘們，那比丘尼犯了波羅夷罪。」

Original (Pali, IAST):

[190] Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū dāyaṃ ālimpesuṃ ; manussā daḍḍhā kālamakaṃsu. Tesaṃ kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, namaraṇādhippāyassā’’ti. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū maraṇādhippāyā dāyaṃ ālimpesuṃ. Manussā daḍḍhā kālamakaṃsu…pe… manussā daḍḍhā na kālamakaṃsu. Tesaṃ kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti thullaccayassā’’ti.

[191] Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu āghātanaṃ gantvā coraghātaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, māyimaṃ kilamesi. Ekena pahārena jīvitā voropehī’’ti. ‘‘Suṭṭhu, bhante’’ti ekena pahārena jīvitā voropesi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu āghātanaṃ gantvā coraghātaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, māyimaṃ kilamesi. Ekena pahārena jīvitā voropehī’’ti. So – ‘‘nāhaṃ tuyhaṃ vacanaṃ karissāmī’’ti taṃ jīvitā voropesi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhu, pārājikassa; āpatti dukkaṭassā’’ti.

[192] Tena kho pana samayena aññataro puriso ñātighare hatthapādacchinno ñātakehi samparikiṇṇo hoti. Aññataro bhikkhu te manusse etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, icchatha imassa maraṇa’’nti? ‘‘Āma, bhante, icchāmā’’ti. ‘‘Tena hi takkaṃ pāyethā’’ti. Te taṃ takkaṃ pāyesuṃ. So kālamakāsi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena aññataro puriso kulaghare hatthapādacchinno ñātakehi samparikiṇṇo hoti. Aññatarā bhikkhunī te manusse etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, icchatha imassa maraṇa’’nti? ‘‘Āmayye, icchāmā’’ti. ‘‘Tena hi loṇasovīrakaṃ pāyethā’’ti. Te taṃ loṇasovīrakaṃ pāyesuṃ. So kālamakāsi. Tassā kukkuccaṃ ahosi. Atha kho sā bhikkhunī bhikkhunīnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesi. Bhikkhuniyo bhikkhūnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Āpattiṃ sā, bhikkhave, bhikkhunī āpannā pārājika’’nti.
