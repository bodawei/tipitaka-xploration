Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 208-209

[208] In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the second jhāna"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the second jhāna"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the second jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat… In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the third jhāna"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the third jhāna"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the third jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the fourth jhāna"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the fourth jhāna"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the fourth jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the empty liberation"… "the first jhāna and the signless liberation"… "the first jhāna and the undirected liberation"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the undirected liberation"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the undirected liberation have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the empty concentration"… "the first jhāna and the signless concentration"… "the first jhāna and the undirected concentration"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the undirected concentration"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the undirected concentration have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the empty attainment"… "the first jhāna and the signless attainment"… "the first jhāna and the undirected attainment"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the undirected attainment"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the undirected attainment have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the three true knowledges"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the three true knowledges"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the three true knowledges have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the four foundations of mindfulness"… "the first jhāna and the four right strivings"… "the first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the five faculties"… "the first jhāna and the five powers"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the five powers"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the five powers have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat.

[209] In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the noble eightfold path"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and the noble eightfold path"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and the noble eightfold path have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the fruit of stream-entry"… "the first jhāna and the fruit of once-returning"… "the first jhāna and the fruit of non-returning"… "the first jhāna and arahantship"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna and arahantship"… "I am a master of them"… "The first jhāna and arahantship have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the first jhāna"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna"… "I am a master of it"… "The first jhāna has been realised by me, and lust has been given up by me"… "and hatred has been given up by me"… "and delusion has been given up by me, vomited forth, released, abandoned, relinquished, cast out, utterly cast out" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways… in seven ways: "I attained the first jhāna"… "I am attaining"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the first jhāna"… "I am a master of it"… "The first jhāna has been realised by me, and my mind is unobstructed by lust"… "and my mind is unobstructed by hatred"… "and my mind is unobstructed by delusion" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. Beforehand it occurs to him, "I shall speak falsely"; while speaking it occurs to him, "I am speaking falsely"; when he has spoken it occurs to him, "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.
