Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 210-213

[210] In three ways: "I attained the second jhāna and the third jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the second jhāna and the third jhāna"… "I am master of them"… "The second jhāna and the third jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the second jhāna and the fourth jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the second jhāna and the fourth jhāna"… "I am master of them"… "The second jhāna and the fourth jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the second jhāna and the empty liberation"… the signless liberation… the undirected liberation… the empty concentration… the signless concentration… the undirected concentration… the empty attainment… the signless attainment… the undirected attainment… the three true knowledges… the four foundations of mindfulness… the four right strivings… the four bases of psychic power… the five faculties… the five powers… the seven factors of enlightenment… the noble eightfold path… the fruit of stream-entry… the fruit of once-returning… the fruit of non-returning… and arahantship — "I attained"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the second jhāna and arahantship"… "I am master of them"… "The second jhāna and arahantship have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the second jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the second jhāna"… "I am master of it"… "The second jhāna has been realised by me, and lust has been relinquished by me"… "and hatred has been relinquished by me"… "and delusion has been relinquished by me, vomited up, released, abandoned, given up, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust"… "and my mind is unobstructed by hatred"… "and my mind is unobstructed by delusion" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways… and so on… in seven ways: "I attained the second jhāna and the first jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the second jhāna and the first jhāna"… "I am master of them"… "The second jhāna and the first jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat… and so on… he misrepresents his state. In this way, making each one in turn the root, the bound wheel is to be made to revolve.

[211] In three ways: "The third jhāna and the fourth jhāna"… and so on… "I attained the third jhāna and arahantship"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the third jhāna and arahantship"… "I am master of them"… "The third jhāna and arahantship have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "I attained the third jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the third jhāna"… "I am master of it"… "The third jhāna has been realised by me, and lust has been relinquished by me"… "and hatred has been relinquished by me"… "and delusion has been relinquished by me, vomited up, released, abandoned, given up, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust"… "and my mind is unobstructed by hatred"… "and my mind is unobstructed by delusion" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "The third jhāna and the first jhāna"… "I attained the third jhāna and the second jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "I am one who obtains the third jhāna and the second jhāna"… "I am master of them"… "The third jhāna and the second jhāna have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the first jhāna"… and so on… "the second jhāna"… "the third jhāna"… "the fourth jhāna"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the fourth jhāna"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the fourth jhāna has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat.

[212] In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the empty liberation"… the signless liberation… "the undirected liberation"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the undirected liberation"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the undirected liberation has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the empty concentration"… the signless concentration… "the undirected concentration"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the undirected concentration"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the undirected concentration has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the empty attainment"… the signless attainment… "the undirected attainment"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the undirected attainment"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the undirected attainment has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the three true knowledges"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the three true knowledges"… "I am master of them"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the three true knowledges have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the four foundations of mindfulness"… the four right strivings… "the four bases of psychic power"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the four bases of psychic power"… "I am master of them"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the four bases of psychic power have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat.

[213] In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the five faculties"… "the five powers"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the five powers"… "I am master of them"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the five powers have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the seven factors of enlightenment"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the seven factors of enlightenment"… "I am master of them"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the seven factors of enlightenment have been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the noble eightfold path"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains the noble eightfold path"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and the noble eightfold path has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the fruit of stream-entry"… the fruit of once-returning… the fruit of non-returning… "arahantship"… "I attain"… "I have attained"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I am one who obtains arahantship"… "I am master of it"… "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and arahantship has been realised by me" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and lust has been relinquished by me"… "and hatred has been relinquished by me"… "and delusion has been relinquished by me, vomited up, released, abandoned, given up, cast off, utterly cast off" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways… and so on… in seven ways: "My mind is unobstructed by delusion, and my mind is unobstructed by lust"… "and my mind is unobstructed by hatred" — for one speaking thus with deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: beforehand it occurs to him, "I shall speak falsely"; while speaking it occurs to him, "I am speaking falsely"; when he has spoken it occurs to him, "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state. Just as the set with one root has been expounded in full, so too the set with two roots and the rest should be expounded in full.
