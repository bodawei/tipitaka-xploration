Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 214

[214] In three ways … and so on … in seven ways: "I attained the first jhāna and the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna and the empty liberation and the signless liberation and the undirected liberation and the empty concentration and the signless concentration and the undirected concentration and the empty attainment and the signless attainment and the undirected attainment and the three true knowledges and the four foundations of mindfulness and the four right strivings and the four bases of psychic power and the five faculties and the five powers and the seven factors of enlightenment and the noble eightfold path and the fruit of stream-entry and the fruit of once-returning and the fruit of non-returning and arahantship" … "I am attaining" … "I have attained" … and so on … "Lust has been relinquished by me, hatred has been relinquished by me, delusion has been relinquished by me — vomited up, released, abandoned, given up, cast off, utterly cast off. My mind is unobstructed as to lust, my mind is unobstructed as to hatred, my mind is unobstructed as to delusion": when he speaks thus, deliberately lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. Beforehand it occurs to him, "I shall speak falsehood"; while speaking it occurs to him, "I am speaking falsehood"; when he has spoken it occurs to him, "falsehood has been spoken by me"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.

----------------------------------------
Traditional Chinese:

[214] 以三種行相……乃至……以七種行相，說「我已入初禪、第二禪、第三禪、第四禪，空解脫、無相解脫、無願解脫，空三昧、無相三昧、無願三昧，空等至、無相等至、無願等至，三明，四念處，四正勤，四神足，五根，五力，七覺支，八聖道，須陀洹果、斯陀含果、阿那含果、阿羅漢果」……「我今入」……「我已入」……乃至……「我之貪已捨，我之瞋已捨，我之癡已捨，已吐、已脫、已斷、已捨遣、已除去、已全除去。我心離貪之蓋障，我心離瞋之蓋障，我心離癡之蓋障」——如是明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷。所謂：事前即有「我將說妄語」之心，說時有「我正說妄語」之心，說已有「我已說妄語」之心，違隱其見，違隱其忍可，違隱其意樂，違隱其實況。

Original (Pali, IAST):

[214] Tīhākārehi …pe… sattahākārehi paṭhamañca jhānaṃ dutiyañca jhānaṃ tatiyañca jhānaṃ catutthañca jhānaṃ suññatañca vimokkhaṃ animittañca vimokkhaṃ appaṇihitañca vimokkhaṃ suññatañca samādhiṃ animittañca samādhiṃ appaṇihitañca samādhiṃ suññatañca samāpattiṃ animittañca samāpattiṃ appaṇihitañca samāpattiṃ tisso ca vijjā cattāro ca satipaṭṭhāne cattāro ca sammappadhāne cattāro ca iddhipāde pañca ca indriyāni pañca ca balāni satta ca bojjhaṅge ariyañca aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ sotāpattiphalañca sakadāgāmiphalañca anāgāmiphalañca arahattañca samāpajjiṃ… samāpajjāmi… samāpanno…pe… rāgo ca me catto, doso ca me catto, moho ca me catto vanto mutto pahīno paṭinissaṭṭho ukkheṭito samukkheṭito. Rāgā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, dosā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa. Pubbevassa hoti musā bhaṇissanti, bhaṇantassa hoti musā bhaṇāmīti, bhaṇitassa hoti musā mayā bhaṇitanti, vinidhāya diṭṭhiṃ, vinidhāya khantiṃ, vinidhāya ruciṃ, vinidhāya bhāvaṃ.
