Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 216-217

[216] In three modes: wishing to say "I attained the second jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the third jhāna": if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. In three modes: wishing to say "I attained the second jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the fourth jhāna" … and so on … "My mind is unobstructed by delusion": if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. In three modes … and so on … in seven modes: wishing to say "I attained the second jhāna," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the first jhāna": if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence … and so on … he misrepresents his state.

[217] In three modes: wishing to say "My mind is unobstructed by delusion," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "I attained the first jhāna": if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. In three modes … and so on … in seven modes: wishing to say "My mind is unobstructed by delusion," he deliberately speaks the falsehood "My mind is unobstructed by hatred": if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence … and so on … he misrepresents his state.

Traditional Chinese:

[216] 以三行相，欲說「我入第二禪」而說「我入第三禪」，若故妄語，對方領解者得波羅夷罪；對方不領解者得偷蘭遮。以三行相，欲說「我入第二禪」而說「我入第四禪」……乃至……「我心離癡蓋障」，若故妄語，對方領解者得波羅夷罪；對方不領解者得偷蘭遮。以三行相……乃至……以七行相，欲說「我入第二禪」而說「我入初禪」，若故妄語，對方領解者得波羅夷罪；對方不領解者得偷蘭遮……乃至……違隱其實況。

[217] 以三行相，欲說「我心離癡蓋障」而說「我入初禪」，若故妄語，對方領解者得波羅夷罪；對方不領解者得偷蘭遮。以三行相……乃至……以七行相，欲說「我心離癡蓋障」而說「我心離瞋蓋障」，若故妄語，對方領解者得波羅夷罪；對方不領解者得偷蘭遮……乃至……違隱其實況。

Original (Pali, IAST):

[216] Tīhākārehi dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti vattukāmo tatiyaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa paṭivijānantassa āpatti pārājikassa; na paṭivijānantassa āpatti thullaccayassa. Tīhākārehi dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti vattukāmo catutthaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti…pe… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa paṭivijānantassa āpatti pārājikassa; na paṭivijānantassa āpatti thullaccayassa. Tīhākārehi…pe… sattahākārehi dutiyaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti vattukāmo paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa paṭivijānantassa āpatti pārājikassa; na paṭivijānantassa āpatti thullaccayassa…pe… vinidhāya bhāvaṃ.

[217] Tīhākārehi mohā me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti vattukāmo paṭhamaṃ jhānaṃ samāpajjinti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa paṭivijānantassa āpatti pārājikassa; na paṭivijānantassa āpatti thullaccayassa. Tīhākārehi…pe… sattahākārehi mohā me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti vattukāmo dosā me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa paṭivijānantassa āpatti pārājikassa; na paṭivijānantassa āpatti thullaccayassa…pe… vinidhāya bhāvaṃ.
