Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 218-219

[218] By three factors … by seven factors, wishing to say, "I attained the first jhāna and the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna and the empty liberation and the signless liberation and the undirected liberation and the empty concentration and the signless concentration and the undirected concentration and the empty attainment and the signless attainment and the undirected attainment and the three true knowledges and the four foundations of mindfulness and the four right strivings and the four bases of psychic power and the five faculties and the five powers and the seven factors of enlightenment and the noble eightfold path and the fruit of stream-entry and the fruit of once-returning and the fruit of non-returning and arahantship" … "and lust has been renounced by me … and hatred has been renounced by me … and delusion has been renounced by me, vomited up, let go, abandoned, relinquished, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust … and my mind is unobstructed by hatred" — if he says, deliberately lying, "My mind is unobstructed by delusion," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence.

[219] By three factors, wishing to say, "I attained the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna and the empty liberation and the signless liberation and the undirected liberation and the empty concentration and the signless concentration and the undirected concentration and the empty attainment and the signless attainment and the undirected attainment and the three true knowledges and the four foundations of mindfulness and the four right strivings and the four bases of psychic power and the five faculties and the five powers and the seven factors of enlightenment and the noble eightfold path and the fruit of stream-entry and the fruit of once-returning and the fruit of non-returning and arahantship" … "and lust has been renounced by me … and hatred has been renounced by me … and delusion has been renounced by me, vomited up, let go, abandoned, relinquished, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust … and my mind is unobstructed by hatred … and my mind is unobstructed by delusion" — if he says, deliberately lying, "I attained the first jhāna," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three factors, wishing to say, "the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna … and my mind is unobstructed by delusion and I attained the first jhāna" — if he says, deliberately lying, "I attained the second jhāna," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. By three factors … by seven factors, wishing to say, "and my mind is unobstructed by delusion, and I attained the first jhāna and the second jhāna and the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna … and my mind is unobstructed by lust" — if he says, deliberately lying, "My mind is unobstructed by hatred," then, if the other understands, there is an offence entailing defeat; if the other does not understand, there is a grave offence. Beforehand it occurs to him, "I shall speak falsely"; while speaking it occurs to him, "I am speaking falsely"; when he has spoken it occurs to him, "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.

Traditional Chinese:

[218] 以三種行相……乃至……以七種行相，〔自稱〕：「我已入初禪、第二禪、第三禪、第四禪，入空解脫、無相解脫、無願解脫，入空三昧、無相三昧、無願三昧，入空等至、無相等至、無願等至，〔得〕三明、四念處、四正勤、四神足、五根、五力、七覺支、八聖道、須陀洹果、斯陀含果、阿那含果、阿羅漢果」……乃至……「我已捨貪……我已捨瞋……我已捨癡，已吐、已脫、已斷、已捨遣、已除棄、已全除棄。我心離貪蓋障……我心離瞋蓋障」——欲如是說，而〔說〕「我心離癡蓋障」，如是故妄語者：對方領解，犯波羅夷；對方不領解，犯偷蘭遮。

[219] 以三種行相，〔自稱〕：「我已入第二禪、第三禪、第四禪，入空解脫、無相解脫、無願解脫，入空三昧、無相三昧、無願三昧，入空等至、無相等至、無願等至，〔得〕三明、四念處、四正勤、四神足、五根、五力、七覺支、八聖道、須陀洹果、斯陀含果、阿那含果、阿羅漢果」……乃至……「我已捨貪……我已捨瞋……我已捨癡，已吐、已脫、已斷、已捨遣、已除棄、已全除棄。我心離貪蓋障……我心離瞋蓋障……我心離癡蓋障」——欲如是說，而〔說〕「我已入初禪」，如是故妄語者：對方領解，犯波羅夷；對方不領解，犯偷蘭遮。以三種行相，〔欲說〕「我已入第三禪、第四禪」……乃至……「我心離癡蓋障，我已入初禪」——欲如是說，而〔說〕「我已入第二禪」，如是故妄語者：對方領解，犯波羅夷；對方不領解，犯偷蘭遮。以三種行相……乃至……以七種行相，〔欲說〕「我心離癡蓋障，我已入初禪、第二禪、第三禪、第四禪」……乃至……「我心離貪蓋障」——欲如是說，而〔說〕「我心離瞋蓋障」，如是故妄語者：對方領解，犯波羅夷；對方不領解，犯偷蘭遮。〔所謂七種行相者：〕先前即有「我將說妄語」之心，正說時有「我正說妄語」之心，說已有「我已說妄語」之心，違隱其見，違隱其忍可，違隱其意樂，違隱其實況。
