[180] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. Out of compassion the bhikkhus praised the beauty of death to him. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse: "The Blessed One has laid down a training rule. Have we perhaps incurred an offence entailing defeat?" They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus." Now at that time a certain alms-gathering bhikkhu, sitting down on a chair, crushed and killed a child hidden under a rag. "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat. But one should not sit down on a seat without inspecting it; whoever does so incurs an offence of wrong-doing."
