Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ

[152] At that time a certain bhikkhu was travelling along a high road together with a caravan. A certain man, having enticed that bhikkhu with goods, and seeing the toll-station, gave that bhikkhu a valuable jewel: "Pass this jewel, venerable sir, through the toll-station." Then that bhikkhu passed that jewel through the toll-station. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu."

[153] At that time a certain bhikkhu, out of compassion, released a pig that was caught in a snare. He was troubled by remorse … "With what intention, bhikkhus?" "With the intention of compassion, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one with the intention of compassion." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, released a pig caught in a snare, thinking, "Before the owners see it." He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu, out of compassion, released a deer caught in a snare … with thievish mind, released a deer caught in a snare, thinking, "Before the owners see it" … out of compassion, released fish caught in a fish-trap … with thievish mind, released fish caught in a fish-trap, thinking, "Before the owners see it." He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu, seeing goods in a cart, thinking, "Taking from here I shall become one who has committed an offence entailing defeat," went past, turned it around, and took it. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu took a piece of meat dropped by a hawk, thinking, "I will give it to the owners." The owners accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one without thievish mind." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, took a piece of meat dropped by a hawk, thinking, "Before the owners see it." The owners accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu."

[154] At that time people bound a raft and were floating it down the Aciravatī river. When the binding broke, the timbers scattered and drifted away. Bhikkhus, perceiving them as rags from a dust-heap, hauled them out. The owners accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one perceiving them as rags from a dust-heap." At that time people bound a raft and were floating it down the Aciravatī river. When the binding broke, the timbers scattered and drifted away. Bhikkhus, with thievish mind, hauled them out, thinking, "Before the owners see it." The owners accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus." At that time a certain cowherd hung his cloak on a tree and went away to relieve himself. A certain bhikkhu, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, took it. Then that cowherd accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap." At that time, as a certain bhikkhu was crossing a river, a cloak that had slipped from the hands of some dyers clung to his foot. That bhikkhu took it, thinking, "I will give it to the owners." The owners accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one without thievish mind." At that time, as a certain bhikkhu was crossing a river, a cloak that had slipped from the hands of some dyers clung to his foot. That bhikkhu, with thievish mind, took it, thinking, "Before the owners see it." The owners accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu."

[155] At that time a certain bhikkhu, seeing a jar of ghee, used it little by little. He was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is an offence of wrong-doing." At that time a number of bhikkhus made an arrangement and went off, saying, "We will steal goods." One of them stole the goods. They said thus: "We are not guilty of an offence entailing defeat. The one by whom it was stolen is guilty of an offence entailing defeat." They reported this matter to the Blessed One … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus." At that time a number of bhikkhus made an arrangement, stole goods, and divided them. As they were being divided, each one's portion did not amount to five māsakas, so they said thus: "We are not guilty of an offence entailing defeat." They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus." At that time a certain bhikkhu, during a famine in Sāvatthī, with thievish mind stole a handful of rice from a shopkeeper. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu, during a famine in Sāvatthī, with thievish mind stole a handful of mung beans from a shopkeeper … a handful of beans … a handful of sesame. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time in the Blind Forest at Sāvatthī robbers killed a cow, ate the meat, put away the remainder, and went off. Bhikkhus, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, had it handed over and ate it. The robbers accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap." At that time in the Blind Forest at Sāvatthī robbers killed a pig, ate the meat, put away the remainder, and went off. Bhikkhus, perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap, had it handed over and ate it. The robbers accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one perceiving it as a rag from a dust-heap." At that time a certain bhikkhu went to a grass field and, with thievish mind, stole cut grass worth five māsakas. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu went to a grass field, cut grass, and, with thievish mind, stole it, worth five māsakas. He was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu."

[156] At that time visiting bhikkhus had the Saṅgha's mangoes divided and ate them. The resident bhikkhus accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … they reported this matter to the Blessed One. "With what intention, bhikkhus?" "For the purpose of eating, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for the purpose of eating." At that time visiting bhikkhus had the Saṅgha's rose-apples … the Saṅgha's jujubes … the Saṅgha's jackfruits … the Saṅgha's palm fruits … the Saṅgha's sugar-cane … the Saṅgha's wood-apples divided and ate them. The resident bhikkhus accused those bhikkhus: "You are no recluses." They were troubled by remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for the purpose of eating." At that time mango-guardians gave mango fruits to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus, scrupulous, thinking, "These masters are guarding them; these are not for giving," did not accept them. They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, in the giving of a guardian." At that time rose-apple guardians … jujube guardians … jackfruit guardians … palm-fruit guardians … sugar-cane guardians … wood-apple guardians gave wood-apples to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus, scrupulous, thinking, "These masters are guarding them; these are not for giving," did not accept them. They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, in the giving of a guardian." At that time a certain bhikkhu, having taken the Saṅgha's timber on temporary borrowing, propped up the wall of his own dwelling. The bhikkhus accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." He was troubled by remorse. He reported this matter to the Blessed One. "With what intention, bhikkhu?" "On temporary borrowing, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, in the case of temporary borrowing." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, stole the Saṅgha's water … with thievish mind, stole the Saṅgha's clay … with thievish mind, stole the Saṅgha's piled-up grass … he was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, burned the Saṅgha's piled-up grass. He was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu. There is an offence of wrong-doing." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, stole the Saṅgha's couch … he was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." At that time a certain bhikkhu, with thievish mind, stole the Saṅgha's chair … the Saṅgha's mattress … the Saṅgha's pillow … the Saṅgha's door-panel … the Saṅgha's window-lattice … the Saṅgha's door-bolt … he was troubled by remorse … "You have committed an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu."

[157] At that time bhikkhus were using, elsewhere, a dwelling-place that was for the use of a certain lay follower's dwelling. Then that lay follower complained, grumbled, and spread it about: "How can the venerable ones use elsewhere what is for use elsewhere!" They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "Bhikkhus, what is for use elsewhere should not be used elsewhere. Whoever should use it commits an offence of wrong-doing." At that time bhikkhus, scrupulous about carrying seats to the uposatha hall and to the assembly, sat on the ground. Their limbs and robes became dusty. They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "I allow, bhikkhus, carrying them on temporary borrowing." At that time at Campā the pupil bhikkhunī of the bhikkhunī Thullanandā went to the family that supported the bhikkhunī Thullanandā and said, "The noble lady wishes to drink three-ingredient gruel." Having had it cooked and carried away, she ate it herself. The latter, knowing of it, accused her: "You are no recluse." She was troubled by remorse. Then that bhikkhunī reported this matter to the bhikkhunīs. The bhikkhunīs reported this matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is an offence of expiation for deliberate lying." At that time at Rājagaha the pupil bhikkhunī of the bhikkhunī Thullanandā went to the family that supported the bhikkhunī Thullanandā and said, "The noble lady wishes to eat honey-balls." Having had them cooked and carried away, she ate them herself. The latter, knowing of it, accused her: "You are no recluse." She was troubled by remorse … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is an offence of expiation for deliberate lying."

[158] At that time at Vesālī the householder who supported the Venerable Ajjuka had two children: a son and a sister's son. Then that householder said to the Venerable Ajjuka: "Venerable sir, whichever of these two children is faithful and devoted, to him you should assign this position." At that time that householder's sister's son was faithful and devoted. Then the Venerable Ajjuka assigned that position to that child. With that wealth he established the family and established giving. Then that householder's son said to the Venerable Ānanda: "Venerable Ānanda, who is heir to the father — the son or the sister's son?" "The son, friend, is heir to the father." "Venerable sir, this noble Ajjuka assigned our wealth to one who is our male relative." "Friend, the Venerable Ajjuka is no recluse." Then the Venerable Ajjuka said to the Venerable Ānanda: "Give me a decision, friend Ānanda." At that time the Venerable Upāli was of the Venerable Ajjuka's party. Then the Venerable Upāli said to the Venerable Ānanda: "Friend Ānanda, when one has been told by the owner, 'Assign this position to such-and-such,' and he assigns it to him, what does he commit?" "He commits nothing, venerable sir, not even so much as an offence of wrong-doing." "Friend, this Venerable Ajjuka was told by the owner, 'Assign this position to such-and-such,' and he assigned it to him; there is no offence, friend, for the Venerable Ajjuka."

[159] At that time at Bārāṇasī the family that supported the Venerable Pilindavaccha was harassed by robbers. And two children were carried off. Then the Venerable Pilindavaccha, by psychic power, brought those children back and placed them in the palace. People, seeing those children, said, "This is the Venerable Pilindavaccha's power of psychic accomplishment," and they gained great faith in the Venerable Pilindavaccha. The bhikkhus complained, grumbled, and spread it about: "How can the Venerable Pilindavaccha bring back children carried off by robbers!" They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one with psychic power within the range of psychic power."

[160] At that time two bhikkhus were companions — Paṇḍuka and Kapila. One dwelt in a village, one at Kosambī. Then, as that bhikkhu was going from the village to Kosambī and crossing a river on the way, a roll of fat that had slipped from the hands of some pig-butchers clung to his foot. That bhikkhu took it, thinking, "I will give it to the owners." The owners accused that bhikkhu: "You are no recluse." A cowherd woman, seeing him after he had crossed, said to him: "Come, venerable sir, engage in sexual intercourse." He, thinking, "I am in fact no recluse anyway," engaged in sexual intercourse with her, and having gone to Kosambī, reported this matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus, in the matter of taking what is not given; there is an offence entailing defeat for the coupling in sexual intercourse."

[161] At that time at Sāgala a bhikkhu, a co-resident pupil of the Venerable Daḷhika, oppressed by discontent, stole a shopkeeper's turban and said to the Venerable Daḷhika: "Venerable sir, I am no recluse; I will leave the Order." "What have you done, friend?" He reported the matter. He had it brought and valued. When valued, it did not amount to five māsakas. "There is no offence entailing defeat, friend." He gave a Dhamma talk. That bhikkhu delighted in it.

[152] 那時，有一位比丘與商隊一同行於道路。有一個男子以財物引誘那位比丘，看見稅關之後，便將一顆貴重的摩尼寶珠交給那位比丘：「尊者，請您將這顆寶珠帶過稅關。」於是那位比丘便將那顆寶珠帶過了稅關。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」

[153] 那時，有一位比丘以悲憫之心解開了一隻被繩索綁住的豬。他心生追悔……「比丘，你是以什麼心念？」「世尊，我是出於悲憫之意。」「比丘，出於悲憫之意者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘解開了一隻被繩索綁住的豬，心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心而解開。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位比丘以悲憫之心解開了一隻被繩索綁住的鹿……他解開了一隻被繩索綁住的鹿，心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心而解開……他以悲憫之心解開了困在籠中的魚……他解開了困在籠中的魚，心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心而解開。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位比丘看見車上的貨物，心想「若從這裡拿取，我便成波羅夷」，便越過之後再翻轉過來拿取。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一塊肉片被鷹叼起，一位比丘心想「我要還給物主」而拿取。物主們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔……「比丘，無盜心者無罪。」那時，有一塊肉片被鷹叼起，一位比丘心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心而拿取。物主們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」

[154] 那時，人們將木筏綁好，順著阿奇拉瓦底河漂流而下。綁繩斷了，木頭散落各處而去。比丘們作糞掃衣想，將它們撈了上來。物主們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……「諸比丘，作糞掃衣想者無罪。」那時，人們將木筏綁好，順著阿奇拉瓦底河漂流而下。綁繩斷了，木頭散落各處而去。比丘們心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心將它們撈了上來。物主們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……「諸比丘，你們犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位牧牛人將上衣掛在樹上，前去解手。有一位比丘作糞掃衣想而拿取。於是那位牧牛人責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔……「比丘，作糞掃衣想者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘渡河時，一件從洗衣人手中脫落的衣服掛在他的腳上。那位比丘心想「我要還給物主」而拿取。物主們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔……「比丘，無盜心者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘渡河時，一件從洗衣人手中脫落的衣服掛在他的腳上。那位比丘心想「在物主看見之前」，以盜心而拿取。物主們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」

[155] 那時，有一位比丘看見一甕酥，便一點一點地食用。他心生追悔……「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯突吉羅罪。」那時，眾多比丘商議之後一同前去：「我們要盜取貨物。」其中一人盜取了貨物。他們這樣說：「我們不是波羅夷；盜取者才是波羅夷。」他們將此事稟告世尊……「諸比丘，你們犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，眾多比丘商議之後盜取貨物，然後分贓。分贓時，每人所分不到五摩沙迦。他們這樣說：「我們不是波羅夷。」他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，你們犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，在舍衛城饑饉之時，有一位比丘以盜心盜取了店家的一把米。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，在舍衛城饑饉之時，有一位比丘以盜心盜取了店家的一把綠豆……一把豆……一把芝麻。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，在舍衛城安達林中的盜賊們殺了一頭牛，吃了肉，將剩餘的藏好後離去。比丘們作糞掃衣想，令人受取之後食用了。盜賊們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……「諸比丘，作糞掃衣想者無罪。」那時，在舍衛城安達林中的盜賊們殺了一頭豬，吃了肉，將剩餘的藏好後離去。比丘們作糞掃衣想，令人受取之後食用了。盜賊們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……「諸比丘，作糞掃衣想者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘前往草地，以盜心盜取了已割下的、值五摩沙迦的草。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位比丘前往草地，割了草之後，以盜心盜取了值五摩沙迦的草。他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」

[156] 那時，客比丘們令人分取僧伽的芒果而食用。舊住比丘們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，你們是以什麼心念？」「世尊，我們是為了食用。」「諸比丘，為了食用者無罪。」那時，客比丘們令人分取僧伽的閻浮果……僧伽的葫蘆……僧伽的波羅蜜……僧伽的糖棕果……僧伽的甘蔗……僧伽的餘甘子而食用。舊住比丘們責罵那些比丘：「你們不是沙門。」他們心生追悔……「諸比丘，為了食用者無罪。」那時，看守芒果的人將芒果布施給比丘們。比丘們心想「這些是守護者的，不是他們所能給的」，心生疑悔而不接受。他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，守護者布施者無罪。」那時，看守閻浮果者……看守葫蘆者……看守波羅蜜者……看守糖棕果者……看守甘蔗者……看守餘甘子者將餘甘子布施給比丘們。比丘們心想「這些是守護者的，不是他們所能給的」，心生疑悔而不接受。他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，守護者布施者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘將僧伽的木料暫時取用，用來支撐自己住處的牆壁。比丘們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」他心生追悔，將此事稟告世尊。「比丘，你是以什麼心念？」「世尊，我是暫時取用。」「比丘，暫時取用者無罪。」那時，有一位比丘以盜心盜取了僧伽的水……以盜心盜取了僧伽的泥土……以盜心盜取了僧伽堆積的草……他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位比丘以盜心燒了僧伽堆積的草。他心生追悔……「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯突吉羅罪。」那時，有一位比丘以盜心盜取了僧伽的床……他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位比丘以盜心盜取了僧伽的椅……僧伽的褥……僧伽的枕……僧伽的門扇……僧伽的窗欞……僧伽的門栓……他心生追悔……「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」

[157] 那時，比丘們將某位優婆塞供養作為住處受用的臥坐具，拿到別處受用。於是那位優婆塞抱怨、非難、責備：「諸位大德怎能將別處受用之物拿到別處受用呢！」他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，別處受用之物不應拿到別處受用。若受用者，犯突吉羅罪。」那時，比丘們因為顧慮不能將（坐具）搬到布薩堂及聚會處，便坐在地上。身體與衣服都沾滿了塵土。他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，我允許暫時搬動。」

那時，在瞻波城，偷蘭難陀比丘尼的弟子比丘尼前往偷蘭難陀比丘尼的施主家，說：「阿夷想喝三匙粥。」煮好之後，拿來自己食用了。她（偷蘭難陀）知道後責罵她：「你不是沙門尼。」她心生追悔。於是那位比丘尼將此事告訴比丘尼們。比丘尼們將此事告訴比丘們。比丘們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯故妄語波逸提罪。」那時，在王舍城，偷蘭難陀比丘尼的弟子比丘尼前往偷蘭難陀比丘尼的施主家，說：「阿夷想吃蜜丸。」煮好之後，拿來自己食用了。她（偷蘭難陀）知道後責罵她：「你不是沙門尼。」她心生追悔……「諸比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯故妄語波逸提罪。」

[158] 那時，在毘舍離，具壽阿鳩卡（Ajuka）的施主家主有兩個孩子——一個兒子與一個外甥。於是那位家主對具壽阿鳩卡這樣說：「尊者，關於這兩個孩子中，誰有信仰、有信心，請您就將這個（繼承的）機會告訴他。」那時，那位家主的外甥有信仰、有信心。於是具壽阿鳩卡便將那個機會告訴了那個孩子。他以此財富建立了家業，並設立了布施。於是那位家主的兒子對具壽阿難這樣說：「尊者阿難，誰是父親的繼承人——兒子還是外甥？」「友，兒子是父親的繼承人。」「尊者，這位阿鳩卡尊者把我們的家產告訴了我們的（競爭者）那位有淫欲關係的人。」「友，具壽阿鳩卡不是沙門。」於是具壽阿鳩卡對具壽阿難這樣說：「友阿難，請給我一個裁決。」那時，具壽優波離是具壽阿鳩卡的一方。於是具壽優波離對具壽阿難這樣說：「友阿難，若有人受物主所說『請將這個機會告訴某某』而告訴他，他犯什麼罪？」「尊者，他什麼也不犯，乃至連突吉羅都不犯。」「友，這位具壽阿鳩卡是受物主所說『請將這個機會告訴某某』而告訴他；友，具壽阿鳩卡無罪。」

[159] 那時，在波羅奈，具壽畢陵陀婆蹉的施主家被盜賊所擾害。兩個孩子被擄走。於是具壽畢陵陀婆蹉以神通將那些孩子帶來，安置在樓閣上。人們看見那些孩子，說：「這是畢陵陀婆蹉尊者的神通威力。」於是對具壽畢陵陀婆蹉深生信心。比丘們抱怨、非難、責備：「具壽畢陵陀婆蹉怎能以神通將盜賊擄走的孩子帶回來呢！」他們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，具神通者於神通範圍內無罪。」

[160] 那時，有兩位比丘是朋友——般陀迦與迦毗羅。一位住在村中，一位住在拘睒彌。於是那位從村中前往拘睒彌的比丘，途中渡河時，一塊從獵豬人手中脫落的肥肉團掛在他的腳上。那位比丘心想「我要還給物主」而拿取。物主們責罵那位比丘：「你不是沙門。」一位牧牛女看見他上岸，這樣說：「來吧，尊者，行淫法吧。」他心想「我本來就不是沙門」，便與她行了淫法，然後前往拘睒彌，將此事告訴比丘們。比丘們將此事稟告世尊。「諸比丘，於不與取無波羅夷罪；於行淫法之結合犯波羅夷罪。」

[161] 那時，在沙竭羅，具壽達利迦（Daḷhika）的共住弟子比丘為不喜樂所逼迫，盜取了店家的布，然後對具壽達利迦這樣說：「尊者，我不是沙門，我要還俗。」「友，你做了什麼？」他將此事說了出來。他（達利迦）令人取來估價。估價的結果不值五摩沙迦。「友，於波羅夷無罪。」他為他說法。那位比丘便歡喜而住了。

Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti sampajānamusāvāde pācittiyassā’’ti. Tena kho pana samayena rājagahe thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā antevāsinī bhikkhunī thullanandāya bhikkhuniyā upaṭṭhākakulaṃ gantvā – ‘‘ayyā icchati madhugoḷakaṃ khāditu’’nti, pacāpetvā haritvā attanā paribhuñji. Sā jānitvā taṃ codesi – ‘‘assamaṇīsi tva’’nti. Tassā kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti sampajānamusāvāde pācittiyassā’’ti.

[158] Tena kho pana samayena vesāliyaṃ āyasmato ajjukassa upaṭṭhākassa gahapatino dve dārakā honti – putto ca bhāgineyyo ca. Atha kho so gahapati āyasmantaṃ ajjukaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘imaṃ, bhante, okāsaṃ yo imesaṃ dvinnaṃ dārakānaṃ saddho hoti pasanno tassa ācikkheyyāsī’’ti . Tena kho pana samayena tassa gahapatino bhāgineyyo saddho hoti pasanno. Atha kho āyasmā ajjuko taṃ okāsaṃ tassa dārakassa ācikkhi. So tena sāpateyyena kuṭumbañca saṇṭhapesi dānañca paṭṭhapesi. Atha kho tassa gahapatino putto āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘ko nu kho, bhante ānanda, pituno dāyajjo – putto vā bhāgineyyo vā’’ti? ‘‘Putto kho, āvuso, pituno dāyajjo’’ti. ‘‘Ayaṃ, bhante, ayyo ajjuko amhākaṃ sāpateyyaṃ amhākaṃ methunakassa ācikkhī’’ti. ‘‘Assamaṇo, āvuso, āyasmā ajjuko’’ti. Atha kho āyasmā ajjuko āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘dehi me, āvuso ānanda, vinicchaya’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā upāli āyasmato ajjukassa pakkho hoti. Atha kho āyasmā upāli āyasmantaṃ ānandaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘yo nu kho, āvuso ānanda, sāmikena ‘imaṃ okāsaṃ itthannāmassa ācikkheyyāsī’ti vutto tassa ācikkhati, kiṃ so āpajjatī’’ti? ‘‘Na, bhante, kiñci āpajjati, antamaso dukkaṭamattampī’’ti. ‘‘Ayaṃ, āvuso, āyasmā ajjuko sāmikena – ‘imaṃ okāsaṃ itthannāmassa ācikkhā’ti vutto tassa ācikkhati; anāpatti, āvuso, āyasmato ajjukassā’’ti.

[159] Tena kho pana samayena bārāṇasiyaṃ āyasmato pilindavacchassa upaṭṭhākakulaṃ corehi upaddutaṃ hoti. Dve ca dārakā nītā honti. Atha kho āyasmā pilindavaccho te dārake iddhiyā ānetvā pāsāde ṭhapesi. Manussā te dārake passitvā – ‘‘ayyassāyaṃ pilindavacchassa iddhānubhāvo’’ti, āyasmante pilindavacche abhippasīdiṃsu. Bhikkhū ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti – ‘‘kathañhi nāma āyasmā pilindavaccho corehi nīte dārake ānessatī’’ti! Bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave , iddhimassa iddhivisaye’’ti.

[160] Tena kho pana samayena dve bhikkhū sahāyakā honti – paṇḍuko ca kapilo ca. Eko gāmake viharati, eko kosambiyaṃ. Atha kho tassa bhikkhuno gāmakā kosambiṃ gacchantassa antarāmagge nadiṃ tarantassa sūkarikānaṃ hatthato muttā medavaṭṭi pāde laggā hoti. So bhikkhu – ‘‘sāmikānaṃ dassāmī’’ti aggahesi. Sāmikā taṃ bhikkhuṃ codesuṃ – ‘‘assamaṇosi tva’’nti. Taṃ uttiṇṇaṃ gopālikā passitvā etadavoca – ‘‘ehi, bhante, methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevā’’ti. So – ‘‘pakatiyāpāhaṃ assamaṇo’’ti tassā methunaṃ dhammaṃ paṭisevitvā kosambiṃ gantvā bhikkhūnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesi. Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ orocesuṃ. ‘‘Anāpatti, bhikkhave, adinnādāne pārājikassa; āpatti methunadhammasamāyoge pārājikassā’’ti.

[161] Tena kho pana samayena sāgalāyaṃ āyasmato daḷhikassa saddhivihāriko bhikkhu anabhiratiyā pīḷito āpaṇikassa veṭhanaṃ avaharitvā āyasmantaṃ daḷhikaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘assamaṇo ahaṃ, bhante, vibbhamissāmī’’ti. ‘‘Kiṃ tayā, āvuso, kata’’nti? So tamatthaṃ ārocesi. Āharāpetvā agghāpesi. Taṃ agghāpentaṃ na pañcamāsake agghati. ‘‘Anāpatti, āvuso, pārājikassā’’ti. Dhammakathaṃ akāsi. So bhikkhu abhiramatīti .
