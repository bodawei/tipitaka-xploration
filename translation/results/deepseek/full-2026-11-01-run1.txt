Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ

[180] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. Out of compassion the bhikkhus praised the beauty of death to him. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse: "The Blessed One has laid down a training rule. Have we perhaps incurred an offence entailing defeat?" They reported the matter to the Blessed One. "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus." Now at that time a certain alms-gathering bhikkhu, sitting down on a chair, crushed and killed a child hidden under a rag. He had remorse: "The Blessed One has laid down a training rule. Have I perhaps incurred an offence entailing defeat?" He reported the matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat. But one should not sit down on a seat without inspecting it; whoever does so incurs an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, arranging seats in the refectory within the village, took hold of one pestle while the pestles were standing upright. A second pestle fell over and landed on the head of a certain child. The child died. He had remorse ... "With what intention were you, bhikkhu?" "Unintentionally, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, arranging seats in the refectory within the village, stepped on some pots and overturned them. He crushed and killed a certain child. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time a father and son had gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Sangha is waiting for you," and taking him by the back, he pushed him away. He fell and died. He had remorse ... "With what intention were you, bhikkhu?" "I had no intention of causing death, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a father and son had gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Sangha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death, taking him by the back, he pushed him away. He fell and died. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a father and son had gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Sangha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death, taking him by the back, he pushed him away. He fell but did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence."

[181] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, while eating, had a piece of meat caught in his throat. A certain bhikkhu struck him on the neck. Bloody meat came up. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, while eating, had a piece of meat caught in his throat. A certain bhikkhu, with the intention of causing death, struck him on the neck. Bloody meat came up. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, while eating, had a piece of meat caught in his throat. A certain bhikkhu, with the intention of causing death, struck him on the neck. Bloody meat came up. That bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain alms-gathering bhikkhu received a portion of alms-food that was poisoned, and bringing it back on his return, gave it as the first portion to the bhikkhus. Those bhikkhus died. He had remorse ... "With what intention were you, bhikkhu?" "I did not know, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since you did not know." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, intending to make a test, gave poison to a certain bhikkhu. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "With what intention were you, bhikkhu?" "I was intending to make a test, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence."

[182] Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing building work on a dwelling. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a stone. The stone, badly held by the bhikkhu above, landed on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing building work on a dwelling. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a stone. The bhikkhu above, with the intention of causing death, dropped the stone on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were raising the wall of a dwelling. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a brick. The brick, badly held by the bhikkhu above, landed on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were raising the wall of a dwelling. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a brick. The bhikkhu above, with the intention of causing death, dropped the brick on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence."

[183] Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing new building work. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a chisel. The chisel, badly held by the bhikkhu above, landed on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing new building work. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a chisel. The bhikkhu above, with the intention of causing death, dropped the chisel on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing new building work. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a rafter. The rafter, badly held by the bhikkhu above, landed on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since it was unintentional." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī were doing new building work. A certain bhikkhu, standing below, raised up a rafter. The bhikkhu above, with the intention of causing death, dropped the rafter on the head of the bhikkhu below. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī, doing new building work, were constructing a watchtower. A certain bhikkhu said to a certain bhikkhu: "Friend, stand there and build." While building there, he fell and died. He had remorse ... "With what intention were you, bhikkhu?" "I had no intention of causing death, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time the bhikkhus of Āḷavī, doing new building work, were constructing a watchtower. A certain bhikkhu, with the intention of causing death, said to a certain bhikkhu: "Friend, stand there and build." While building there, he fell and died ... he fell and did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, having roofed a dwelling, was climbing down. A certain bhikkhu said to that bhikkhu: "Friend, climb down here." As he climbed down there, he fell and died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, having roofed a dwelling, was climbing down. A certain bhikkhu, with the intention of causing death, said to that bhikkhu: "Friend, climb down here." As he climbed down there, he fell and died ... he fell and did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, oppressed by discontent, climbed Vulture Peak mountain and, throwing himself off the precipice, crushed and killed a certain basket-maker. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat. But one should not throw oneself down, bhikkhus. Whoever does so incurs an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time the group-of-six bhikkhus climbed Vulture Peak mountain and threw stones down for sport. One of them crushed and killed a certain cowherd. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat. But one should not throw stones down for sport, bhikkhus. Whoever does so incurs an offence of wrong-doing."

[184] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus made him sweat. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, made him sweat. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu had a headache. The bhikkhus gave him a nasal medicine. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu had a headache. The bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, gave him a nasal medicine. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus massaged him. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, massaged him. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus bathed him. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, bathed him. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus anointed him with oil. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, since you had no intention of causing death." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was sick. The bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, anointed him with oil. That bhikkhu died ... that bhikkhu did not die. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence."

[186] Now at that time a certain woman whose husband was away was pregnant by a lover. She said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "Please, venerable sir, find me an abortifacient." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her an abortifacient. The child died. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain man had two wives — one barren, one fertile. The barren woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "If that woman, venerable sir, gives birth, she will be mistress of the whole household. Please, venerable sir, find an abortifacient for her." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her an abortifacient. The child died, but the mother did not die. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain man had two wives — one barren, one fertile. The barren woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "If that woman, venerable sir, gives birth, she will be mistress of the whole household. Please, venerable sir, find an abortifacient for her." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her an abortifacient. The mother died, but the child did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain man had two wives — one barren, one fertile. The barren woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "If that woman, venerable sir, gives birth, she will be mistress of the whole household. Please, venerable sir, find an abortifacient for her." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her an abortifacient. Both died ... both did not die. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence."

[187] Now at that time a certain pregnant woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "Please, venerable sir, find me an abortifacient." "Then, sister, have yourself pressed," he said. She had herself pressed and brought about an abortion. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain pregnant woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "Please, venerable sir, find me an abortifacient." "Then, sister, have yourself heated," he said. She had herself heated and brought about an abortion. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain barren woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "Please, venerable sir, find me a medicine by which I may give birth." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her a medicine. She died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time a certain fertile woman said to a bhikkhu who frequented the family: "Please, venerable sir, find me a medicine by which I may not give birth." "Very well, sister," he said, and gave her a medicine. She died. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is an offence of wrong-doing." Now at that time the group-of-six bhikkhus made a bhikkhu of the group of seventeen laugh with a poke of the finger. That bhikkhu, choking, unable to catch his breath, died. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat." Now at that time the bhikkhus of the group of seventeen, saying "We will perform a formal act against the group-of-six bhikkhus," crushed and killed a bhikkhu of the group of six. They had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhus, entailing defeat." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu who was a spirit-doctor deprived a yakkha of life. He had remorse ... "There is no offence, bhikkhu, entailing defeat; there is a grave offence." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu sent a certain bhikkhu to a haunt of fierce yakkhas. The yakkhas deprived him of life. Now at that time a certain bhikkhu sent a certain bhikkhu into a wilderness of wild beasts. The wild beasts deprived him of life. He had remorse ... [188] Now at that time a certain bhikkhu, thinking him to be that one, deprived that one of life ... thinking him to be that one, he deprived another of life ... thinking him to be another, he deprived that one of life ... thinking him to be another, he deprived another of life. He had remorse ... "You have incurred an offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu." Now at that time a certain bhikkhu was seized by a non-human being. A certain bhikkhu struck that bhikkhu. [180] 那時，某比丘生病。諸比丘出於悲憫，向他讚歎死之美好。那位比丘命終。諸比丘心生追悔：「世尊已制學處，我們是否犯了波羅夷罪？」他們將此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「諸比丘，你們犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，某位入村乞食的比丘，坐在覆蓋著破布的小凳上，未察覺而壓死了布下的小孩。他心生追悔：「世尊已制學處，我是否犯了波羅夷罪？」他將此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪。諸比丘，然而不可未察看即坐於座位上；若坐者，犯突吉羅罪。」那時，某比丘在食堂內為人敷設座位時，杵臼豎立著，他拿取了一根杵。第二根杵倒下，落在某個小孩頭上。那小孩命終。他心生追悔……世尊問：「比丘，你當時是什麼心？」他答：「世尊，我並非故意。」世尊說：「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，某比丘在食堂內為人敷設座位時，踏到臼具，使之滾動，壓死了某個小孩。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，有父子二人皆出家為比丘。到了通知時，兒子對父親說：「去吧，長老，僧眾在等你。」他抓住父親的背，將他推倒。父親跌倒而命終。他心生追悔……世尊問：「比丘，你當時是什麼心？」他答：「世尊，我沒有致死的意圖。」世尊說：「比丘，無致死意圖者無罪。」那時，有父子二人皆出家為比丘。到了通知時，兒子對父親說：「去吧，長老，僧眾在等你。」他懷著致死意圖抓住父親的背，將他推倒。父親跌倒而命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，有父子二人皆出家為比丘。到了通知時，兒子對父親說：「去吧，長老，僧眾在等你。」他懷著致死意圖抓住父親的背，將他推倒。父親跌倒但未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯偷蘭遮罪。」

[182] 那時，阿羅毗的諸比丘建造寺院。某比丘在下方，向上遞石。上方比丘失手，石頭落在下方比丘頭上。那位比丘命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，阿羅毗的諸比丘建造寺院。某比丘在下方，向上遞石。上方比丘懷著致死意圖，鬆手讓石頭落在下方比丘頭上。那位比丘命終……那位比丘未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯偷蘭遮罪。」那時，阿羅毗的諸比丘砌寺院牆。某比丘在下方，向上遞磚。上方比丘失手，磚落在下方比丘頭上。那位比丘命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，非故意者無罪。」那時，阿羅毗的諸比丘砌寺院牆。某比丘在下方，向上遞磚。上方比丘懷著致死意圖，鬆手讓磚落在下方比丘頭上。那位比丘命終……那位比丘未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯偷蘭遮罪。」

[186] 那時，某婦人丈夫外出，與他人私通而懷孕。她對常來家中的比丘說：「尊者，請您設法為我墮胎。」比丘說：「好的，姊妹。」便給她墮胎之藥。胎兒命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，某男子有兩位妻子——一位不孕，一位有生育。不孕的婦人對常來家中的比丘說：「尊者，若她生產，她將成為整個家宅之主。尊者，請您設法為她墮胎。」比丘說：「好的，姊妹。」便給她墮胎之藥。胎兒命終，母親未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」那時，某男子有兩位妻子——一位不孕，一位有生育。不孕的婦人對常來家中的比丘說：「尊者，若她生產，她將成為整個家宅之主。尊者，請您設法為她墮胎。」比丘說：「好的，姊妹。」便給她墮胎之藥。母親命終，胎兒未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯偷蘭遮罪。」那時，某男子有兩位妻子——一位不孕，一位有生育。不孕的婦人對常來家中的比丘說：「尊者，若她生產，她將成為整個家宅之主。尊者，請您設法為她墮胎。」比丘說：「好的，姊妹。」便給她墮胎之藥。母子二人皆命終……母子二人皆未命終。他心生追悔……世尊說：「比丘，於波羅夷無罪；犯偷蘭遮罪。」
