Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 190-192

[190] At that time the group-of-six bhikkhus set fire to a forest tract; people were burned and died. They had remorse … "There is no offence, bhikkhus, for one who had no intention of causing death." At that time the group-of-six bhikkhus, with the intention of causing death, set fire to a forest tract. People were burned and died … people were burned but did not die. They had remorse … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhus; there is a grave offence."

[191] At that time a certain bhikkhu went to the place of execution and said to the executioner: "Friend, do not torment this man. Deprive him of life with a single blow." "Very well, venerable sir," he deprived him of life with a single blow. He had remorse … "You, bhikkhu, have incurred an offence entailing defeat." At that time a certain bhikkhu went to the place of execution and said to the executioner: "Friend, do not torment this man. Deprive him of life with a single blow." He thought, "I will not do what you say," and deprived him of life. He had remorse … "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is an offence of wrong-doing."

[192] At that time a certain man in his relatives' house had his hands and feet cut off and was surrounded by his kinsfolk. A certain bhikkhu said to those people: "Friends, do you wish for this man's death?" "Yes, venerable sir, we wish it." "Then give him buttermilk to drink." They gave him that buttermilk. He died. He had remorse … "You, bhikkhu, have incurred an offence entailing defeat." At that time a certain man in a family's house had his hands and feet cut off and was surrounded by his kinsfolk. A certain bhikkhunī said to those people: "Friends, do you wish for this man's death?" "Yes, lady, we wish it." "Then give him salt-sovīraka to drink." They gave him that salt-sovīraka. He died. She had remorse. Then that bhikkhunī reported the matter to the bhikkhunīs. The bhikkhunīs reported the matter to the bhikkhus. The bhikkhus reported the matter to the Blessed One. "That bhikkhunī, bhikkhus, has incurred an offence entailing defeat."

Traditional Chinese:

[190] 當時，六群比丘塗抹林地（使地面焦黑）；人們被燒而喪命。他們心生追悔……（世尊說：）「諸比丘，若無致死之意圖，則無罪。」當時，六群比丘懷致死之意圖而塗抹林地。人們被燒而喪命……人們被燒而未喪命。他們心生追悔……（世尊說：）「諸比丘，不成波羅夷；犯偷蘭遮。」

[191] 當時，有一位比丘前往刑場，對行刑的劊子手說：「賢友，不要讓此人受苦，一刀斷其性命吧。」（劊子手答：）「好的，尊者。」便一刀斷其性命。他（比丘）心生追悔……（世尊說：）「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」當時，有一位比丘前往刑場，對行刑的劊子手說：「賢友，不要讓此人受苦，一刀斷其性命吧。」他（劊子手）說：「我不聽你的話。」便斷其性命。他（比丘）心生追悔……（世尊說：）「比丘，不成波羅夷；犯突吉羅。」

[192] 當時，有一人在親族家中被截斷手足，親族圍繞照料。有一位比丘對那些人說：「賢友們，你們希望此人死嗎？」（他們答：）「是的，尊者，我們希望。」（比丘說：）「那麼就給他喝酸粥（takka）吧。」他們便給他喝酸粥。那人因此喪命。他（比丘）心生追悔……（世尊說：）「比丘，你犯了波羅夷罪。」當時，有一人在族人家中被截斷手足，親族圍繞照料。有一位比丘尼對那些人說：「賢友們，你們希望此人死嗎？」（他們答：）「是的，阿夷，我們希望。」（比丘尼說：）「那麼就給他喝鹽酸粥（loṇasovīraka）吧。」他們便給他喝鹽酸粥。那人因此喪命。她（比丘尼）心生追悔。於是那位比丘尼將此事稟告諸比丘尼。諸比丘尼將此事稟告諸比丘。諸比丘將此事稟告世尊。（世尊說：）「諸比丘，那位比丘尼犯了波羅夷罪。」

Original (Pali, IAST):

[190] Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū dāyaṃ ālimpesuṃ ; manussā daḍḍhā kālamakaṃsu. Tesaṃ kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, namaraṇādhippāyassā’’ti. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū maraṇādhippāyā dāyaṃ ālimpesuṃ. Manussā daḍḍhā kālamakaṃsu…pe… manussā daḍḍhā na kālamakaṃsu. Tesaṃ kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhave, pārājikassa; āpatti thullaccayassā’’ti.

[191] Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu āghātanaṃ gantvā coraghātaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, māyimaṃ kilamesi. Ekena pahārena jīvitā voropehī’’ti. ‘‘Suṭṭhu, bhante’’ti ekena pahārena jīvitā voropesi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena aññataro bhikkhu āghātanaṃ gantvā coraghātaṃ etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, māyimaṃ kilamesi. Ekena pahārena jīvitā voropehī’’ti. So – ‘‘nāhaṃ tuyhaṃ vacanaṃ karissāmī’’ti taṃ jīvitā voropesi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘anāpatti, bhikkhu, pārājikassa; āpatti dukkaṭassā’’ti.

[192] Tena kho pana samayena aññataro puriso ñātighare hatthapādacchinno ñātakehi samparikiṇṇo hoti. Aññataro bhikkhu te manusse etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, icchatha imassa maraṇa’’nti? ‘‘Āma, bhante, icchāmā’’ti. ‘‘Tena hi takkaṃ pāyethā’’ti. Te taṃ takkaṃ pāyesuṃ. So kālamakāsi. Tassa kukkuccaṃ ahosi…pe… ‘‘āpattiṃ tvaṃ, bhikkhu, āpanno pārājika’’nti. Tena kho pana samayena aññataro puriso kulaghare hatthapādacchinno ñātakehi samparikiṇṇo hoti. Aññatarā bhikkhunī te manusse etadavoca – ‘‘āvuso, icchatha imassa maraṇa’’nti? ‘‘Āmayye, icchāmā’’ti. ‘‘Tena hi loṇasovīrakaṃ pāyethā’’ti. Te taṃ loṇasovīrakaṃ pāyesuṃ. So kālamakāsi. Tassā kukkuccaṃ ahosi. Atha kho sā bhikkhunī bhikkhunīnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesi. Bhikkhuniyo bhikkhūnaṃ etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. Bhikkhū bhagavato etamatthaṃ ārocesuṃ. ‘‘Āpattiṃ sā, bhikkhave, bhikkhunī āpannā pārājika’’nti.
