Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 193-202

[193] At that time the Buddha, the Blessed One, was dwelling at Vesālī in the Great Wood, in the Hall with the Peaked Roof. Now at that time a number of bhikkhus who were friends and companions, having entered upon the rains retreat on the bank of the river Vaggumudā. Now at that time the Vajjī country was short of food, stricken by famine, with white bones scattered about and provisions scarce; it was not easy to keep going by gleaning and by begging. Then this thought occurred to those bhikkhus: "Now the Vajjī country is short of food, stricken by famine, with white bones scattered about and provisions scarce; it is not easy to keep going by gleaning and by begging. By what means might we dwell for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and not be wearied by almsfood?" Some said this: "Come, friends, let us take on the work of the householders; then they will think they should give to us. Thus we shall dwell for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and not be wearied by almsfood." Some said this: "Enough, friends, of taking on the work of the householders! Come, friends, let us carry messages for the householders; then they will think they should give to us. Thus we shall dwell for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and not be wearied by almsfood." Some said this: "Enough, friends, of taking on the work of the householders! What need of carrying messages for the householders? Come, friends, let us speak to the householders in praise of one another's superhuman states: 'Such and such a bhikkhu is an obtainer of the first jhāna; such and such a bhikkhu is an obtainer of the second jhāna; such and such a bhikkhu is an obtainer of the third jhāna; such and such a bhikkhu is an obtainer of the fourth jhāna; such and such a bhikkhu is a stream-enterer; such and such a bhikkhu is a once-returner; such and such a bhikkhu is a non-returner; such and such a bhikkhu is an arahant; such and such a bhikkhu is one with the three true knowledges; such and such a bhikkhu is one with the six higher knowledges.' Then they will think they should give to us. Thus we shall dwell for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and not be wearied by almsfood." "This is the best, friends — that we speak to the householders in praise of one another's superhuman states."

[194] Then those bhikkhus spoke to the householders in praise of one another's superhuman states: "Such and such a bhikkhu is an obtainer of the first jhāna … such and such a bhikkhu is one with the six higher knowledges." Then those people thought: "We are fortunate, we have gained much, that such bhikkhus have entered upon the rains retreat among us; never before have such bhikkhus, virtuous and of good character, entered upon the rains retreat among us." And they did not themselves eat such foods as they gave to the bhikkhus; they gave to their parents, gave to their wives and children, gave to their slaves, servants and workers, gave to their friends and companions, gave to their relatives and kin — such foods as they gave to the bhikkhus. And they did not themselves eat, taste or drink such hard foods, savoury foods and drinks as they gave to the bhikkhus; they gave to their parents, gave to their wives and children, gave to their slaves, servants and workers, gave to their friends and companions, gave to their relatives and kin — such as they gave to the bhikkhus. Then those bhikkhus became handsome, with full faculties, with bright and clear complexions, with radiant and clear skin. Now it was the custom for bhikkhus who had completed the rains retreat to go to see the Blessed One. Then those bhikkhus, having completed the rains retreat and after the passing of three months, set their lodgings in order, took their bowls and robes, and set out for Vesālī. In due course they came to Vesālī, to the Great Wood, to the Hall with the Peaked Roof, to the Blessed One; having approached, they paid homage to the Blessed One and sat down to one side. Now at that time bhikkhus who had completed the rains retreat in other regions were thin, rough, ill-favoured, pale and sallow, with veins standing out on their bodies; but the bhikkhus from the bank of the Vaggumudā were handsome, with full faculties, with bright and clear complexions, with radiant and clear skin. Now it was the custom for the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, to exchange greetings with incoming bhikkhus. Then the Blessed One said this to the bhikkhus from the bank of the Vaggumudā: "I hope, bhikkhus, that you are keeping well, that you are getting by; I hope you dwelt for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and were not wearied by almsfood?" "We are keeping well, Blessed One, we are getting by, Blessed One. And we, venerable sir, dwelt for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and were not wearied by almsfood." The Tathāgatas ask even though they know, and do not ask even though they know … the Buddhas, the Blessed Ones, question bhikkhus for two reasons: that they may teach the Dhamma, or that they may lay down a training rule for the disciples. Then the Blessed One said this to the bhikkhus from the bank of the Vaggumudā: "How was it, bhikkhus, that you dwelt for the rains retreat in concord, rejoicing together, without dispute, and at ease, and were not wearied by almsfood?" Then those bhikkhus told this matter to the Blessed One. "I hope, bhikkhus, that this is true?" "It is not true, Blessed One." The Buddha, the Blessed One, rebuked them: "It is unseemly, foolish men, it is not in conformity, it is not fitting, it is not worthy of a recluse, it is not allowable, it is not to be done. How could you, foolish men, for the sake of the belly, speak to the householders in praise of one another's superhuman states! It were better for you, foolish men, that your bellies be cut open with a sharp butcher's knife, than that you, for the sake of the belly, speak to the householders in praise of one another's superhuman states! What is the reason for that? Because from that cause, foolish men, one might meet with death or with suffering near to death, but one would not thereby, at the breaking up of the body after death, be reborn in a state of deprivation, a bad destination, perdition, hell. But from this cause, foolish men, at the breaking up of the body after death, one would be reborn in a state of deprivation, a bad destination, perdition, hell. This, foolish men, is not for the gaining of confidence in the unconvinced" … and having rebuked them, having given a Dhamma talk, the Blessed One addressed the bhikkhus:

[195] "Bhikkhus, there are these five great thieves existing in the world. What five? Here, bhikkhus, it occurs to a certain great thief: 'When shall I, surrounded by a hundred or a thousand, wander through villages, towns and royal capitals, slaying, slaughtering, cutting, having others cut, cooking, having others cook!' And after some time, surrounded by a hundred or a thousand, he wanders through villages, towns and royal capitals, slaying, slaughtering, cutting, having others cut, cooking, having others cook. In just the same way, bhikkhus, it occurs to a certain wicked bhikkhu here: 'When shall I, surrounded by a hundred or a thousand, walk on tour through villages, towns and royal capitals, honoured, esteemed, revered, venerated and respected by householders and by those gone forth, and become an obtainer of robes, almsfood, lodging, and medicinal requisites for the sick!' And after some time, surrounded by a hundred or a thousand, he walks on tour through villages, towns and royal capitals, honoured, esteemed, revered, venerated and respected by householders and by those gone forth, and becomes an obtainer of robes, almsfood, lodging, and medicinal requisites for the sick. This, bhikkhus, is the first great thief existing in the world. "Furthermore, bhikkhus, a certain wicked bhikkhu here, having learned the Dhamma and Discipline proclaimed by the Tathāgata, keeps it for himself. This, bhikkhus, is the second great thief existing in the world. "Furthermore, bhikkhus, a certain wicked bhikkhu here defames a pure one who is living the holy life, wholly pure, with a groundless charge of unchastity. This, bhikkhus, is the third great thief existing in the world. "Furthermore, bhikkhus, a certain wicked bhikkhu here, with those heavy properties and heavy requisites of the Saṅgha — that is, a park, park-land, a monastery, monastery-land, a couch, a chair, a mattress, a pillow, a copper pot, a copper vessel, a copper ladle, a copper cauldron, an adze, an axe, a hatchet, a hoe, a spade, a creeper, bamboo, muñja grass, pabbaja grass, grass, clay, wooden goods, clay goods — with these he wins over and coaxes householders. This, bhikkhus, is the fourth great thief existing in the world. "In the world with its devas, bhikkhus, with its Māras, with its Brahmās, among the population with its recluses and brahmins, its devas and humans, this is the foremost great thief: he who proclaims a non-existent, untrue superhuman state. What is the reason for that? Because, bhikkhus, the country's almsfood has been eaten by you by way of theft." Then the Blessed One, having rebuked those bhikkhus from the bank of the Vaggumudā in many ways for being hard to support, hard to maintain … and said: "And so, bhikkhus, let this training rule be rehearsed — 'Whatever bhikkhu, not knowing, should declare to himself a superhuman state, knowledge and vision worthy of the noble ones, saying "Thus I know, thus I see," and afterwards, whether he is being cross-questioned or not, being guilty and looking for purification, should say, "Not knowing, friends, I said I knew; not seeing, I said I saw — I prattled empty falsehood" — he too is one who has committed an offence entailing defeat, not in communion." Thus this training rule was laid down by the Blessed One for the bhikkhus.

[196] Now at that time a number of bhikkhus, perceiving what was unseen as seen, perceiving what was unattained as attained, perceiving what was unachieved as achieved, perceiving what was unrealized as realized, declared final knowledge through overestimation. Afterwards their minds inclined to lust, their minds inclined to hatred, their minds inclined to delusion. They had remorse: "The Blessed One has laid down a training rule. We, perceiving what was unseen as seen, perceiving what was unattained as attained, perceiving what was unachieved as achieved, perceiving what was unrealized as realized, declared final knowledge through overestimation. Are we perhaps guilty of an offence entailing defeat?" They told this matter to the venerable Ānanda. The venerable Ānanda told this matter to the Blessed One. "There are, Ānanda, those bhikkhus who, perceiving what was unseen as seen, perceiving what was unattained as attained, perceiving what was unachieved as achieved, perceiving what was unrealized as realized, declare final knowledge through overestimation. But that is not to be reckoned as an offence." "And so, bhikkhus, let this training rule be rehearsed —

[197] 'Whatever bhikkhu, not knowing, should declare to himself a superhuman state, knowledge and vision worthy of the noble ones, saying "Thus I know, thus I see," and afterwards, whether he is being cross-questioned or not, being guilty and looking for purification, should say, "Not knowing, friends, I said I knew; not seeing, I said I saw — I prattled empty falsehood" — unless it be through overestimation — he too is one who has committed an offence entailing defeat, not in communion.'"

[198] 'Whatever' means whatever kind … 'bhikkhu' means … this is the bhikkhu intended in this case. 'Not knowing' means not knowing, not seeing, a wholesome state in himself that is non-existent, untrue, not present, thinking 'I have a wholesome state.' 'Superhuman state' means jhāna, liberation, concentration, attainment, knowledge and vision, development of the path, realization of the fruits, abandonment of the defilements, the unobstructed state of mind, delight in an empty hut. 'To himself' means he applies those wholesome states to himself, or applies himself to those wholesome states. 'Knowledge' means the three true knowledges. 'Vision' means whatever is knowledge, that is vision; whatever is vision, that is knowledge. 'Should declare' means he should announce to a woman or a man, to a householder or to one gone forth. 'Thus I know, thus I see' means I know these states, I see these states; these states exist in me, and I am found among these states. 'Afterwards' means when that moment, that instant, that brief time in which the declaration was made has passed. 'Being cross-questioned' means being formally examined regarding the matter that was claimed: 'What have you attained? How have you attained it? When have you attained it? Where have you attained it? Which defilements have you abandoned? Of which states are you an obtainer?' 'Not being cross-questioned' means not being spoken to by anyone. 'Guilty' means one of evil desires, overcome by desire, having proclaimed a non-existent, untrue superhuman state, he has committed an offence entailing defeat. 'Looking for purification' means wishing to be a householder, or wishing to be a lay follower, or wishing to be a park-attendant, or wishing to be a novice. 'Not knowing, friends, I said I knew; not seeing, I said I saw' means I do not know these states, I do not see these states; these states do not exist in me, and I am not found among these states. 'I prattled empty falsehood' means it was said by me emptily, it was said by me falsely, it was said by me untruthfully, it was said by me not knowing. 'Unless it be through overestimation' means setting aside overestimation. 'Too' means it is said in reference to the former. 'Is one who has committed an offence entailing defeat' means just as a palm tree whose crown is cut off is incapable of growing again, in the same way a bhikkhu of evil desires, overcome by desire, having proclaimed a non-existent, untrue superhuman state, is no longer a recluse, no longer a son of the Sakyans. Therefore it is said, 'he has committed an offence entailing defeat.' 'Not in communion' means communion is a single formal act, a single recitation, an equal training — that is called communion. That he does not have with them. Therefore it is said, 'not in communion.'

[199] 'Superhuman state' means jhāna, liberation, concentration, attainment, knowledge and vision, development of the path, realization of the fruits, abandonment of the defilements, the unobstructed state of mind, delight in an empty hut. 'Jhāna' means the first jhāna, the second jhāna, the third jhāna, the fourth jhāna. 'Liberation' means the empty liberation, the signless liberation, the undirected liberation. 'Concentration' means empty concentration, signless concentration, undirected concentration. 'Attainment' means the emptiness attainment, the signless attainment, the undirected attainment. 'Knowledge and vision' means the three true knowledges. 'Development of the path' means the four foundations of mindfulness, the four right strivings, the four bases of psychic power, the five faculties, the five powers, the seven factors of enlightenment, the noble eightfold path. 'Realization of the fruits' means realization of the fruit of stream-entry, realization of the fruit of once-return, realization of the fruit of non-return, realization of arahantship. 'Abandonment of the defilements' means the abandonment of lust, the abandonment of hatred, the abandonment of delusion. 'The unobstructed state of mind' means the mind's unobstructed state from lust, the mind's unobstructed state from hatred, the mind's unobstructed state from delusion. 'Delight in an empty hut' means delight in an empty hut by the first jhāna, delight in an empty hut by the second jhāna, delight in an empty hut by the third jhāna, delight in an empty hut by the fourth jhāna.

[200] 'In three ways "I attain the first jhāna"' — for one speaking deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking there is in him 'I shall speak falsely'; while speaking there is in him 'I am speaking falsely'; after speaking there is in him 'I have spoken falsely.' 'In four ways "I attain the first jhāna"' — for one speaking deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking there is in him 'I shall speak falsely'; while speaking there is in him 'I am speaking falsely'; after speaking there is in him 'I have spoken falsely'; and he misrepresents his view. 'In five ways "I attain the first jhāna"' — for one speaking deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking there is in him 'I shall speak falsely'; while speaking there is in him 'I am speaking falsely'; after speaking there is in him 'I have spoken falsely'; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief. 'In six ways "I attain the first jhāna"' — for one speaking deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking there is in him 'I shall speak falsely'; while speaking there is in him 'I am speaking falsely'; after speaking there is in him 'I have spoken falsely'; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference. 'In seven ways "I attain the first jhāna"' — for one speaking deliberate lying there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking there is in him 'I shall speak falsely'; while speaking there is in him 'I am speaking falsely'; after speaking there is in him 'I have spoken falsely'; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.

[193] 其時，世尊佛住毘舍離大林之樓閣講堂。爾時，眾多相識相親之諸比丘，於跋求摩河岸入雨安居。其時，跋耆國饑饉，難得之食，白骨之色，憑籌分食，不易以拾取與乞求而得存活。時諸比丘作是念：「今跋耆國饑饉，難得之食，白骨之色，憑籌分食，不易以拾取與乞求而得存活。我等當以何方便，和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，而不以乞食為苦？」有者如是言：「諸賢！我等當教敕在家人之作務，如是彼等當思惟施與我等。如是，我等和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，而不以乞食為苦。」有者如是言：「諸賢！何用教敕在家人之作務為！諸賢！我等當為在家人傳送使命，如是彼等當思惟施與我等。如是，我等和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，而不以乞食為苦。」有者如是言：「諸賢！何用教敕在家人之作務為！何用為在家人傳送使命為！諸賢！我等當互相對在家人稱說上人法：『某比丘得初禪，某比丘得第二禪，某比丘得第三禪，某比丘得第四禪，某比丘是須陀洹，某比丘是斯陀含，某比丘是阿那含，某比丘是阿羅漢，某比丘是三明者，某比丘是六神通者。』如是彼等當思惟施與我等。如是，我等和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，而不以乞食為苦。」「諸賢！唯此為勝，即我等互相對在家人稱說上人法。」

[194] 時彼諸比丘互相對在家人稱說上人法：「某比丘得初禪……某比丘是六神通者。」時彼諸人曰：「我等實得善利，我等實得善得，我等處有如是諸比丘入雨安居；我等以前未嘗有如是有戒、有善法之諸比丘入雨安居。」彼等於自所受用之飲食不食，而施與父母、施與妻子、施與奴婢傭工、施與親友、施與親族血親，唯如是以施與諸比丘。彼等於自所嚼所嚐所飲之嚼食、噉食、飲品不嚼不嚐不飲，而施與父母、施與妻子、施與奴婢傭工、施與親友、施與親族血親，唯如是以施與諸比丘。時彼諸比丘顏色光澤，諸根肥滿，面容明淨，膚色皎潔。然雨安居訖之諸比丘，慣例往見世尊。時彼諸比丘雨安居訖，過三月已，整備臥坐處，持缽與衣，往毘舍離。次第至毘舍離大林樓閣講堂，詣世尊所；詣已，頂禮世尊，退坐一面。其時，諸方雨安居訖之諸比丘，消瘦、粗澀、醜陋，色如敗白，身筋脈暴露；而跋求摩河岸之諸比丘，顏色光澤，諸根肥滿，面容明淨，膚色皎潔。然諸佛世尊慣例與新來諸比丘互相問訊。時世尊告跋求摩河岸諸比丘曰：「諸比丘！可堪忍否？可維持否？諸比丘！汝等和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，不以乞食為苦否？」「世尊！可堪忍，世尊！可維持。大德！我等和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，不以乞食為苦。」如來雖知亦問，雖知亦不問……諸佛世尊以二種因緣問諸比丘：當為說法，或當為諸弟子制立學處。時世尊告跋求摩河岸諸比丘曰：「諸比丘！汝等云何和合歡悅、無有諍論，安樂住於雨安居，不以乞食為苦？」時彼諸比丘以是事白世尊。「諸比丘！此事為真實否？」「世尊！不真實。」世尊佛呵責曰：「愚癡人！此不相應、不隨順、不適宜、非沙門法、不可行、不應行。愚癡人！汝等云何為腹之故，互相對在家人稱說上人法！愚癡人！寧可汝等以利刀屠腹，亦不應為腹之故，互相對在家人稱說上人法！何以故？愚癡人！由彼因緣，或當遭遇死亡，或當遭遇如死之劇苦，然不應由彼因緣，身壞命終之後，墮於惡趣、惡道、險難、地獄。愚癡人！由此因緣，則身壞命終之後，墮於惡趣、惡道、險難、地獄。愚癡人！此不能令未信者生信……」……呵責已，說法，告諸比丘：

[195] 「諸比丘！世間現有五种大賊。何等為五？諸比丘！於此，某大賊作是念：『何時我當為百人千人圍繞，遊行於村落、市鎮、王都，殺戮、屠害、截斷、令人截斷、烹煮、令人烹煮！』彼於後時，為百人千人圍繞，遊行於村落、市鎮、王都，殺戮、屠害、截斷、令人截斷、烹煮、令人烹煮。諸比丘！如是於此，某惡比丘作是念：『何時我當為百人千人圍繞，遊行於村落、市鎮、王都，為在家人與出家人所尊重、恭敬、奉事、供養、禮敬，而得衣、缽食、臥坐處、病緣醫藥資具！』彼於後時，為百人千人圍繞，遊行於村落、市鎮、王都，為在家人與出家人所尊重、恭敬、奉事、供養、禮敬，而得衣、缽食、臥坐處、病緣醫藥資具。諸比丘！此世間現有第一種大賊。復次，諸比丘！於此，某惡比丘學得如來所宣說之法與律，而以之為己有。諸比丘！此世間現有第二種大賊。復次，諸比丘！於此，某惡比丘以無根之非梵行事，誹謗清淨、行清淨梵行之梵行者。諸比丘！此世間現有第三種大賊。復次，諸比丘！於此，某惡比丘以僧伽之重物、重資具，所謂園、園地、精舍、精舍地、床、小凳、褥、枕、銅釜、銅甕、銅盆、銅鍋、刀、斧、鉞、鋤、鎬、藤、竹、蘆葦、燈心草、草、泥土、木器、陶器，以此等攝受在家人、令在家人親附。諸比丘！此世間現有第四種大賊。諸比丘！於有諸天、魔、梵之世間，於沙門婆羅門、天人眾中，此為第一大賊，即未得謂得而宣說不實、虛妄之上人法。何以故？諸比丘！汝等以盜心食國土之信施故。」時世尊以種種方便呵責彼跋求摩河岸諸比丘，言其難養、難育……「諸比丘！當如是誦此學處——凡比丘實未了知，而自稱得上人法、足以成聖之智見，言『我如是知、如是見』，彼於後時，或被詰問、或不被詰問，既犯罪而欲求清淨，乃如是言：『諸賢！我實不知而言知，不見而言見，我虛妄而說。』除增上慢，此亦犯波羅夷，不共住。」如是世尊為諸比丘制立學處。

[196] 其時，眾多比丘於未見作已見想，於未得作已得想，於未證作已證想，於未作證作已作證想，以增上慢而為人記說。彼等於後時，心亦向於貪、心亦向於瞋、心亦向於癡。彼等生追悔：「世尊已制立學處。我等於未見作已見想，於未得作已得想，於未證作已證想，於未作證作已作證想，以增上慢而為人記說。我等莫非已犯波羅夷罪？」彼等以是事白具壽阿難。具壽阿難以是事白世尊。「阿難！彼諸比丘於未見作已見想，於未得作已得想，於未證作已證想，於未作證作已作證想，以增上慢而為人記說，確有其事。然此是不成違犯。」「諸比丘！當如是誦此學處——

[197] 凡比丘實未了知，而自稱得上人法、足以成聖之智見，言『我如是知、如是見』，彼於後時，或被詰問、或不被詰問，既犯罪而欲求清淨，乃如是言：『諸賢！我實不知而言知，不見而言見，我虛妄而說。』除增上慢，此亦犯波羅夷，不共住。」

[198] 「凡」者，凡、何等……「比丘」者……此處意指比丘。「未了知」者，於自身無有、不實、不存在之善法，不知不見，謂「我無有善法」。「上人法」者，謂禪、解脫、定、等至、智見、修道、證果、斷煩惱、心離蓋障、於空閑處之喜樂。「足以成聖」者，彼將其善法歸於自身，或將自身歸於彼善法。「智」者，三明。「見」者，凡智即是見，凡見即是智。「自稱」者，向女人或男子、在家人或出家人宣說。「我如是知、如是見」者，謂我知此諸法，我見此諸法，此諸法在於我，我與此諸法相應。「於後時」者，謂自稱之那剎那、那頃、那須臾已過。「被詰問」者，於所曾承認之事而被詰問：「汝得何法？汝如何得？汝何時得？汝何處得？汝斷何煩惱？汝得何諸法？」「不被詰問」者，無人問。「犯罪」者，惡欲、為欲所制，宣說不實、虛妄之上人法已，犯波羅夷罪。「欲求清淨」者，欲作在家人、欲作優婆塞、欲作園民、欲作沙彌。「我實不知而言知，不見而言見」者，謂我不知此諸法，我不見此諸法，此諸法不在於我，我與此諸法不相應。「虛妄而說」者，謂我以空無而說，我以虛妄而說，我以不實而說，我以不知而說。「除增上慢」者，除開增上慢。「此亦」者，對前而言。「犯波羅夷」者，譬如截斷頂端之棕櫚，不能再生長；如是，比丘惡欲、為欲所制，宣說不實、虛妄之上人法已，即非沙門、非釋子。故說「犯波羅夷」。「不共住」者，共住者，謂同一羯磨、同一說戒、同等學處，此名共住。彼與之不共。故說「不共住」。

[199] 「上人法」者，謂禪、解脫、定、等至、智見、修道、證果、斷煩惱、心離蓋障、於空閑處之喜樂。「禪」者，初禪、第二禪、第三禪、第四禪。「解脫」者，空解脫、無相解脫、無願解脫。「定」者，空定、無相定、無願定。「等至」者，空等至、無相等至、無願等至。「智見」者，三明。「修道」者，四念處、四正勤、四神足、五根、五力、七覺支、八聖道。「證果」者，證須陀洹果、證斯陀含果、證阿那含果、證阿羅漢果。「斷煩惱」者，斷貪、斷瞋、斷癡。「心離蓋障」者，心離貪、心離瞋、心離癡。「於空閑處之喜樂」者，以初禪樂住空閑處，以第二禪樂住空閑處，以第三禪樂住空閑處，以第四禪樂住空閑處。

[200] 以三種而「入初禪」者，於故意妄語之人，犯波羅夷罪：於說前即有「我當妄語」之想，正說時有「我正妄語」之想，說已即有「我已妄語」之想。以四種而「入初禪」者，於故意妄語之人，犯波羅夷罪：於說前即有「我當妄語」之想，正說時有「我正妄語」之想，說已即有「我已妄語」之想，並違隱其見。以五種而「入初禪」者，於故意妄語之人，犯波羅夷罪：於說前即有「我當妄語」之想，正說時有「我正妄語」之想，說已即有「我已妄語」之想，並違隱其見、違隱其忍可。以六種而「入初禪」者，於故意妄語之人，犯波羅夷罪：於說前即有「我當妄語」之想，正說時有「我正妄語」之想，說已即有「我已妄語」之想，並違隱其見、違隱其忍可、違隱其意樂。以七種而「入初禪」者，於故意妄語之人，犯波羅夷罪：於說前即有「我當妄語」之想，正說時有「我正妄語」之想，說已即有「我已妄語」之想，並違隱其見、違隱其忍可、違隱其意樂、違隱其實況。
