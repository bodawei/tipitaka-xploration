Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 203-207

[203] In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I am an attainer of the first jhāna," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely." In four ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I am an attainer of the first jhāna," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely"; and he misrepresents his view. In five ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I am an attainer of the first jhāna," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view; he misrepresents his belief. In six ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I am an attainer of the first jhāna," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view; he misrepresents his belief; he misrepresents his preference. In seven ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I am an attainer of the first jhāna," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view; he misrepresents his belief; he misrepresents his preference; he misrepresents his state.

In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the three true knowledges; I am an attainer of the three true knowledges … I am a master of them … the three true knowledges have been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the four foundations of mindfulness … the four right strivings … the four bases of psychic power; I am an attainer of the four bases of psychic power … I am a master of them … the four bases of psychic power have been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the five faculties … the five powers; I am an attainer of the five powers … I am a master of them … the five powers have been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the seven factors of enlightenment; I am an attainer of the seven factors of enlightenment … I am a master of them … the seven factors of enlightenment have been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the noble eightfold path; I am an attainer of the noble eightfold path … I am a master of it … the noble eightfold path has been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "I entered … I enter … I am entered into the fruit of stream-entry … the fruit of once-returning … the fruit of non-returning … arahantship; I am an attainer of arahantship, I am a master of it, arahantship has been realised by me," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "Lust has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "Hatred has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "Delusion has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "My mind is unobstructed by lust," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "My mind is unobstructed by hatred," there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways … up to … in seven ways, for one who speaks deliberate lying, saying "My mind is unobstructed by delusion," there is an offence entailing defeat: before speaking it is present in him as "I will speak falsely"; while speaking it is present in him as "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken it is present in him as "I have spoken falsely"; he misrepresents his view; he misrepresents his belief; he misrepresents his preference; he misrepresents his state.
