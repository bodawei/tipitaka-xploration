Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 208-209

[208] In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the second jhāna … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the second jhāna … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the second jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat … In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the third jhāna … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the third jhāna … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the third jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the fourth jhāna … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the fourth jhāna … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the fourth jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the empty liberation … the first jhāna and the signless liberation … the first jhāna and the undirected liberation … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the undirected liberation … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the undirected liberation have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and empty concentration … the first jhāna and signless concentration … the first jhāna and undirected concentration … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and undirected concentration … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and undirected concentration have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the empty attainment … the first jhāna and the signless attainment … the first jhāna and the undirected attainment … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the undirected attainment … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the undirected attainment have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the three true knowledges … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the three true knowledges … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the three true knowledges have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the four foundations of mindfulness … the first jhāna and the four right strivings … the first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the four bases of psychic power have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the five faculties … the first jhāna and the five powers … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the five powers … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the five powers have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat.

[209] In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the seven factors of enlightenment have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the noble eightfold path … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and the noble eightfold path … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and the noble eightfold path have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna and the fruit of stream-entry … the first jhāna and the fruit of once-returning … the first jhāna and the fruit of non-returning … the first jhāna and arahantship … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna and arahantship … I am a master of them … the first jhāna and arahantship have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I entered the first jhāna … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna … I am a master of it … the first jhāna has been realized by me, and lust has been abandoned, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off by me … and hatred has been abandoned … and delusion has been abandoned, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off by me — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways … up to seven ways I entered the first jhāna … I enter … I have entered … I am one who has obtained the first jhāna … I am a master of it … the first jhāna has been realized by me, and my mind is unobstructed by lust … my mind is unobstructed by hatred … my mind is unobstructed by delusion — for one speaking deliberate lying, there is an offence entailing defeat. Before speaking he has the thought "I shall speak falsely"; while speaking he has the thought "I am speaking falsely"; having spoken he has the thought "I have spoken falsely" — he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state.

[209] 以三種方式（而說）：我證入初禪與七覺支……我正證入……我已證入……我是初禪與七覺支的獲得者……我是其自在者……初禪與七覺支已為我所證——若故意說虛妄語而如此說者，犯波羅夷。以三種方式（而說）：我證入初禪與八聖道……我正證入……我已證入……我是初禪與八聖道的獲得者……我是其自在者……初禪與八聖道已為我所證——若故意說虛妄語而如此說者，犯波羅夷。以三種方式（而說）：初禪與須陀洹果……初禪與斯陀含果……初禪與阿那含果……我證入初禪與阿羅漢果……我正證入……我已證入……我是初禪與阿羅漢果的獲得者……我是其自在者……初禪與阿羅漢果已為我所證——若故意說虛妄語而如此說者，犯波羅夷。以三種方式（而說）：我證入初禪……我正證入……我已證入……我是初禪的獲得者……我是其自在者……初禪已為我所證，而我之貪已捨……我之瞋已捨……我之癡已捨，已吐出、已解脫、已斷除、已棄捨、已拔除、已徹底拔除——若故意說虛妄語而如此說者，犯波羅夷。以三種方式……乃至以七種方式（而說）：我證入初禪……我正證入……我已證入……我是初禪的獲得者……我是其自在者……初禪已為我所證，而我之心已離貪蓋……我之心已離瞋蓋……我之心已離癡蓋——若故意說虛妄語而如此說者，犯波羅夷。於說之前即有「我將說虛妄語」之念，正在說時有「我正說虛妄語」之念，已說之後有「我已說虛妄語」之念，此即違隱其見、違隱其忍可、違隱其意樂、違隱其實況。
