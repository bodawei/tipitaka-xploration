Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 210-213

[210] In three ways I attained the second jhāna and the third jhāna… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the second jhāna and the third jhāna… I am master of them… the second jhāna and the third jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I attained the second jhāna and the fourth jhāna… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the second jhāna and the fourth jhāna… I am master of them… the second jhāna and the fourth jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I attained the second jhāna and the empty liberation… the signless liberation… the undirected liberation… the empty concentration… the signless concentration… the undirected concentration… the empty attainment… the signless attainment… the undirected attainment… the three true knowledges… the four foundations of mindfulness… the four right strivings… the four bases of psychic power… the five faculties… the five powers… the seven factors of enlightenment… the noble eightfold path… the fruit of stream-entry… the fruit of once-returning… the fruit of non-returning… arahantship — I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the second jhāna and arahantship… I am master of them… the second jhāna and arahantship have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I attained the second jhāna… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the second jhāna… I am master of it… the second jhāna has been realized by me, and lust has been abandoned by me… hatred has been abandoned by me… delusion has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off. And through freedom from lust my mind is unobstructed… through freedom from hatred my mind is unobstructed… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways… up to seven ways I attained the second jhāna and the first jhāna… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the second jhāna and the first jhāna… I am master of them… the second jhāna and the first jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat… he misrepresents his state. Thus, having made each one the root, the bound wheel is to be set turning.

[211] In three ways I attained the third jhāna and the fourth jhāna… the third jhāna and arahantship… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the third jhāna and arahantship… I am master of them… the third jhāna and arahantship have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways I attained the third jhāna… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the third jhāna… I am master of it… the third jhāna has been realized by me, and lust has been abandoned by me… hatred has been abandoned by me… delusion has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off. And through freedom from lust my mind is unobstructed… through freedom from hatred my mind is unobstructed… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways the third jhāna and the first jhāna… the third jhāna and the second jhāna I attained… I attain… I have attained… I am one who has obtained the third jhāna and the second jhāna… I am master of them… the third jhāna and the second jhāna have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the first jhāna… the second jhāna… the third jhāna… the fourth jhāna I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the fourth jhāna… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the fourth jhāna has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat.

[212] In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the empty liberation… the signless liberation… the undirected liberation I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the undirected liberation… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the undirected liberation has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the empty concentration… the signless concentration… the undirected concentration I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the undirected concentration… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the undirected concentration has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the empty attainment… the signless attainment… the undirected attainment I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the undirected attainment… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the undirected attainment has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the four foundations of mindfulness… the four right strivings… the four bases of psychic power I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the four bases of psychic power… I am master of them… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the four bases of psychic power have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat.

[213] In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the five faculties… the five powers I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the five powers… I am master of them… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the five powers have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the seven factors of enlightenment I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the seven factors of enlightenment… I am master of them… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the seven factors of enlightenment have been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the noble eightfold path I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained the noble eightfold path… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the noble eightfold path has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and the fruit of stream-entry… the fruit of once-returning… the fruit of non-returning… arahantship I attained… I attain… I have attained… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and I am one who has obtained arahantship… I am master of it… through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and arahantship has been realized by me — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and lust has been abandoned by me… hatred has been abandoned by me… delusion has been abandoned by me, spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. In three ways… up to seven ways, through freedom from delusion my mind is unobstructed, and through freedom from lust my mind is unobstructed… through freedom from hatred my mind is unobstructed — for one speaking deliberate lies thus, there is an offence entailing defeat. Before speaking there is in him "I will speak a lie"; while speaking there is in him "I am speaking a lie"; when spoken there is in him "a lie has been spoken by me"; he misrepresents his view, he misrepresents his belief, he misrepresents his preference, he misrepresents his state. Just as the single-root version is expanded, so too the two-root and the rest are to be expanded.

[210] 以三種方式，我入第二禪與第三禪……我正入……我已入……我證得第二禪與第三禪之利得……我得自在……「第二禪與第三禪已由我親證」——如此明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷罪。以三種方式，我入第二禪與第四禪……我正入……我已入……我證得第二禪與第四禪之利得……我得自在……「第二禪與第四禪已由我親證」——如此明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷罪。以三種方式，我入第二禪與空解脫……無相解脫……無願解脫……空三昧……無相三昧……無願三昧……空等至……無相等至……無願等至……三明……四念處……四正勤……四神足……五根……五力……七覺支……八聖道……須陀洹果……斯陀含果……阿那含果……阿羅漢果……我正入……我已入……我證得第二禪與阿羅漢果之利得……我得自在……「第二禪與阿羅漢果已由我親證」——如此明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷罪。以三種方式，我入第二禪……我正入……我已入……我證得第二禪之利得……我得自在……「第二禪已由我親證，我之貪已捨棄……我之瞋已捨棄……我之癡已捨棄，已吐、已解脫、已斷、已捨遣、已根除、已徹底根除。我心離貪之蓋障……我心離瞋之蓋障……我心離癡之蓋障」——如此明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷罪。以三種方式……乃至以七種方式，我入第二禪與初禪……我正入……我已入……我證得第二禪與初禪之利得……我得自在……「第二禪與初禪已由我親證」——如此明知而說虛妄語者，犯波羅夷罪……乃至違隱其實況。如是每一根本一一作成後，應作輪轉之環。

Tīhākārehi mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ satta ca bojjhaṅge samāpajjiṃ… samāpajjāmi… samāpanno… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ sattannañca bojjhaṅgānaṃ lābhīmhi … vasīmhi… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ satta ca bojjhaṅgā sacchikatā mayāti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa. Tīhākārehi mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ ariyañca aṭṭhaṅgikaṃ maggaṃ samāpajjiṃ… samāpajjāmi… samāpanno… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ ariyassa ca aṭṭhaṅgikassa maggassa lābhīmhi… vasīmhi… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ ariyo ca aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo sacchikato mayāti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa. Tīhākārehi mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ sotāpattiphalañca… sakadāgāmiphalañca… anāgāmiphalañca… arahattañca samāpajjiṃ… samāpajjāmi… samāpanno… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ arahattassa ca lābhīmhi… vasīmhi… mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ arahattañca sacchikataṃ mayāti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa. Tīhākārehi mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, rāgo ca me catto… doso ca me catto… moho ca me catto vanto mutto pahīno paṭinissaṭṭho ukkheṭito samukkheṭitoti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa. Tīhākārehi…pe… sattahākārehi mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ rāgā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ… dosā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa pubbevassa hoti musā bhaṇissanti, bhaṇantassa hoti musā bhaṇāmīti, bhaṇitassa hoti musā mayā bhaṇitanti, vinidhāya diṭṭhiṃ, vinidhāya khantiṃ, vinidhāya ruciṃ, vinidhāya bhāvaṃ. Yathā ekamūlakaṃ vitthāritaṃ evameva dumūlakādipi vitthāretabbaṃ.
