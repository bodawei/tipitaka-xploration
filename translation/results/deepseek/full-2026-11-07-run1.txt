Pārājikapāḷi — 1. Pārājikakaṇḍaṃ
vin01m.mul.xml, verses 214

[214] In three modes … up to … in seven modes, I entered … I enter … I have entered the first jhāna, the second jhāna, the third jhāna, and the fourth jhāna; the empty liberation, the signless liberation, and the undirected liberation; empty concentration, signless concentration, and undirected concentration; empty attainment, signless attainment, and undirected attainment; the three true knowledges; the four foundations of mindfulness; the four right strivings; the four bases of psychic power; the five faculties; the five powers; the seven factors of enlightenment; the noble eightfold path; the fruit of stream-entry, the fruit of once-returning, the fruit of non-returning, and arahantship. And in me, lust has been abandoned; in me, hatred has been abandoned; in me, delusion has been abandoned — spewed out, released, given up, renounced, cast off, utterly cast off. And my mind is unobstructed by lust, unobstructed by hatred, unobstructed by delusion — for one speaking a deliberate lie, there is an offence entailing defeat. Before speaking, it occurs to him: "I will speak a lie"; while speaking, it occurs to him: "I am speaking a lie"; after speaking, it occurs to him: "I have spoken a lie" — by way of misrepresenting his view, misrepresenting his belief, misrepresenting his preference, misrepresenting his state.

Traditional Chinese:

[214] 以三種方式……乃至以七種方式證入初禪、第二禪、第三禪、第四禪，證入空解脫、無相解脫、無願解脫，證入空三昧、無相三昧、無願三昧，證入空等至、無相等至、無願等至，證入三明、四念處、四正勤、四神足、五根、五力、七覺支、八聖道，證入須陀洹果、斯陀含果、阿那含果、阿羅漢果……我正證入……我已證入……乃至我的貪已捨、我的瞋已捨、我的癡已捨，已吐出、已解脫、已斷除、已棄遣、已根除、已徹底根除。我的心離貪蓋障，我的心離瞋蓋障，我的心離癡蓋障——若明知而說虛妄語，犯波羅夷罪。在說之前，他心想「我將說虛妄語」；正在說時，他心想「我說虛妄語」；說完之后，他心想「我已說虛妄語」——這便是違隱其見、違隱其忍可、違隱其意樂、違隱其實況。

Rāgā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, dosā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇaṃ, mohā ca me cittaṃ vinīvaraṇanti sampajānamusā bhaṇantassa āpatti pārājikassa.
