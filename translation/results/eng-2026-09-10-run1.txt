[60] Monks who are his enemies bring a human male… a non-human male… a male animal into a monk's presence and make him sit down upon his genital organ with the anus… with the mouth. If he consents to the entry, consents to its having entered, consents to its remaining there, consents to its withdrawal, there is an offence entailing defeat…and so on… If he does not consent, there is no offence. Monks who are his enemies bring a male animal that is awake… asleep… drunk… insane… heedless… dead and not devoured… dead and mostly not devoured…and so on… there is an offence entailing defeat. They bring one that is dead and mostly devoured into the monk's presence and make it sit down upon his genital organ with the anus… with the mouth. If he consents to the entry, consents to its having entered, consents to its remaining there, consents to its withdrawal, there is a grave offence…and so on… If he does not consent, there is no offence.

[66] If he inserts an orifice into an orifice, there is an offence entailing defeat. If he inserts an orifice into what is not an orifice, there is an offence entailing defeat. If he inserts what is not an orifice into an orifice, there is an offence entailing defeat. If he inserts what is not an orifice into what is not an orifice, there is a grave offence. A monk transgresses with a sleeping monk: if, on waking, the latter consents, both are to be expelled; if, on waking, he does not consent, only the defiler is to be expelled. A monk transgresses with a sleeping novice: if, on waking, the latter consents, both are to be expelled; if, on waking, he does not consent, only the defiler is to be expelled. A novice transgresses with a sleeping monk: if, on waking, the latter consents, both are to be expelled; if, on waking, he does not consent, only the defiler is to be expelled. A novice transgresses with a sleeping novice: if, on waking, the latter consents, both are to be expelled; if, on waking, he does not consent, only the defiler is to be expelled. There is no offence for one who does not know, for one who does not consent, for one who is mad, for one who is deranged in mind, for one overwhelmed by pain, and for the first offender.
