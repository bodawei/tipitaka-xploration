[180] At that time a certain bhikkhu was ill. Out of compassion for him the bhikkhus praised the beauty of death. That bhikkhu died. They had remorse: "A training rule has been laid down by the Blessed One. Can it be that we have fallen into an offence entailing defeat?" They reported this matter to the Blessed One. "Bhikkhus, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." At that time a certain bhikkhu who went for alms, sitting down on a seat on which a child covered with a cloth was lying, crushed it and killed it. He had remorse: "A training rule has been laid down by the Blessed One. Can it be that I have fallen into an offence entailing defeat?" He reported this matter to the Blessed One. "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu. But, bhikkhus, one should not sit down on a seat without first looking; whoever should so sit down, there is an offence of wrong-doing." At that time a certain bhikkhu, while preparing a seat inside a house in a refectory, took hold of one of two pestles that were propped up. The second pestle fell over and came down on the head of a certain child. He died. That bhikkhu had remorse… "What was your intention, bhikkhu?" "I did not act intentionally, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, when it is unintentional." At that time a certain bhikkhu, while preparing a seat inside a house in a refectory, trod on a mortar-block and made it roll. It crushed a certain child and killed it. He had remorse… "There is no offence, bhikkhu, when it is unintentional." At that time a father and son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time for the meal was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and taking him by the back he pushed him along. He fell down and died. He had remorse… "What was your intention, bhikkhu?" "I had no intention of causing death, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who had no intention of causing death." At that time a father and son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time for the meal was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death he took him by the back and pushed him along. He fell down and died. He had remorse… "Bhikkhu, you have fallen into an offence entailing defeat." At that time a father and son had both gone forth among the bhikkhus. When the time for the meal was announced, the son said to his father: "Go, venerable sir, the Saṅgha is waiting for you," and with the intention of causing death he took him by the back and pushed him along. He fell down but did not die. He had remorse… "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is a grave offence."

The meat fell out together with blood. That bhikkhu did not die. He had remorse… "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is a grave offence." At that time a certain bhikkhu who went for alms received almsfood into which poison had got. He took it back to the dwelling and gave the first portion of it to the bhikkhus. Those bhikkhus died. He had remorse… "What was your intention, bhikkhu?" "I did not know, Blessed One." "There is no offence, bhikkhu, for one who did not know." "I intended to make a test, Blessed One." "There is no offence entailing defeat, bhikkhu; there is a grave offence."

[180] 那時，有一位比丘生病。諸比丘出於憐愍而為他讚歎死之美好。那位比丘便命終了。他們心生追悔：「世尊已制立學處，我們莫非已犯波羅夷罪？」於是把此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「諸比丘，你們已犯波羅夷罪。」那時，有一位行乞食的比丘，在一張小凳上坐下，那凳上有一小孩以破布蓋著，他壓在其上而致其死。他心生追悔：「世尊已制立學處，我莫非已犯波羅夷罪？」便把此事稟告世尊。世尊說：「比丘，不犯波羅夷。然而，諸比丘，不得不察看便坐於座上；若坐者，犯突吉羅。」那時，有一位比丘在食堂、在人家屋內鋪設座位時，拿取豎立著的兩根杵中的一根。第二根杵倒下，落在某個小孩的頭上，那小孩便命終了。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你當時是何心？」「世尊，我非故意。」「比丘，非故意者不犯。」那時，有一位比丘在食堂、在人家屋內鋪設座位時，踏到臼具而使之滾動，壓住某個小孩而致其死。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，非故意者不犯。」那時，有父子二人在比丘中出家。告知食時已到時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧眾正等著你呢。」便抓住他的背推他。父親跌倒而命終。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你當時是何心？」「世尊，我並無致死之意圖。」「比丘，無致死之意圖者不犯。」那時，有父子二人在比丘中出家。告知食時已到時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧眾正等著你呢。」他懷著致死之意圖抓住父親的背而推之。父親跌倒而命終。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，你已犯波羅夷罪。」那時，有父子二人在比丘中出家。告知食時已到時，兒子對父親說：「尊者，去吧，僧眾正等著你呢。」他懷著致死之意圖抓住父親的背而推之。父親跌倒但未命終。他心生追悔……乃至……「比丘，不犯波羅夷；犯偷蘭遮。」
